## Type (computed from live site)

- Headings: **Gotham** (Book weight for display, Bold/Black for small headers), UPPERCASE,
  wide letterspacing. H1 ~75px, H2 ~66px.
- Body/prose: **Archer Book** (slab serif), 24px, italic for quotes.
- Nav: Gotham Book 15px uppercase.
- Web-safe demo substitutes (Gotham/Archer are licensed): Montserrat (300/500/700) for
  Gotham, Zilla Slab or Bitter for Archer. Keep uppercase + tracking wide (~0.15em).

## Texture

Dark sections use a subtle tessellated-triangle pattern (niho-taniwha-like) at very low
contrast over `#161516`. Reproduce as CSS (repeating conic/linear gradient triangles at
~3–4% white opacity) — do not lift their asset.
