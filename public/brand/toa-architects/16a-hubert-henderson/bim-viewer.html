Mana — Authority and Status

The development is within the rohe of Ngāti Whātua Ōrākei. Engagement with mana whenua recommended for any resource consent application. The site is within the Tāmaki Collective area.

Whakapapa — Layered Identity

Remuera sits between Maungakiekie (One Tree Hill) and Ōhinerau (Mt Hobson). The whenua slopes towards the Waitematā. Consider orientation and views to acknowledge these ancestral landmarks in the building design.

Taiao — Natural Environment

Tāmaki Ecological District — native planting should include pōhutukawa, tī kōuka, harakeke, and kawakawa. Existing mature trees on site should be retained where possible. Stormwater design should reference natural water flows.

Mauri Tū — Environmental Health

Building materials should minimise embodied carbon. Consider NZ-sourced timber (FSC-certified radiata pine or native timber where sustainably sourced). Passive solar design should be maximised for the northern aspect.

Mahi Toi — Creative Expression

Opportunity for cultural narratives in fence design, entry thresholds, and landscape features. Consult with mana whenua on appropriate design expressions for this rohe.

Tohu — Wayshowing

Shared accessway should include clear wayshowing that acknowledges both the street address and any cultural naming agreed with mana whenua.

Ahi Kā — Living Presence

The four-unit development should support community connection through shared outdoor spaces and visual permeability between units where appropriate.
