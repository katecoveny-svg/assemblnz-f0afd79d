Mana — Authority and Status

The development is within the rohe of Ngāti Whātua Ōrākei. Engagement with mana whenua recommended for any resource consent application. The site is within the Tāmaki Collective area.

Whakapapa — Layered Identity

Remuera sits within the Waitākere Ranges context. The whenua connects to Te Araroa and the wider Tāmaki Makaurau landscape. Consider orientation to acknowledge ancestral landmarks.

Taiao — Natural Environment

Tāmaki Ecological District — native planting should include pōhutukawa, tī kōuka, harakeke, and kawakawa. Existing mature trees on site should be retained where possible.

Mauri Tū — Environmental Health

Building materials should minimise embodied carbon. Consider NZ-sourced timber (FSC-certified radiata pine). Passive solar design should be maximised for the northern aspect.

Mahi Toi — Creative Expression

Opportunity for cultural narratives in fence design, entry thresholds, and landscape features. Consult with mana whenua on appropriate design expressions.

Tohu — Wayshowing

Shared accessway should include clear wayshowing that acknowledges both the street address and any cultural naming agreed with mana whenua.

Ahi Kā — Living Presence

The four-unit development should support community connection through shared outdoor spaces and visual permeability between units where appropriate.

Foundations

Masterspec 2210 — Concrete Foundations
Draft▶
Part 1 — General

Concrete foundations to NZS 3604:2011 for timber-framed buildings on good ground. Concrete strength fc' = 17.5 MPa minimum. Reinforcing to E/AS1 and NZS 3101. Foundations designed for NZS 1170.5 seismic loading, Zone Factor Z = 0.13 (Auckland).

Part 2 — Products

Ready-mix concrete to NZS 3104. Grade 17.5 for footings, Grade 25 for exposed foundations. Reinforcing steel Grade 300E to AS/NZS 4671. DPC membrane 0.25mm polyethylene under slab. Drain coil to NZS 4309 at footing level.

Part 3 — Execution

Excavate to solid bearing. Min 100mm compacted hardfill. Place reinforcing with min 75mm cover to ground. Pour concrete in one operation per section. Cure for minimum 7 days. Strip formwork after 48hrs min. Waterproof below-grade surfaces with bituminous coating.

Timber Framing

Masterspec 2420 — Timber Framing
Draft▶
Part 1 — General

Timber framing to NZS 3604:2011. H1.2 treated framing for walls and floors. H3.2 treated for bottom plates and any timber within 150mm of ground. Structural design to NZS 3603 where NZS 3604 limits exceeded.

Part 2 — Products

MSG8 or better radiata pine. 90×45 studs at 600mm centres. 90×45 top and bottom plates. 190×45 lintels over openings to 1.2m. LVL beams for spans exceeding NZS 3604 tables. Connector plates, framing anchors, and hold-down bolts to manufacturer specifications.

Part 3 — Execution

Pre-nail wall frames on flat, plumb and brace. Max stud spacing 600mm. Double studs at openings. Nailing schedule per NZS 3604 Table 8.18. Bracing lines per engineer's design or NZS 3604 Chapter 5. All cuts and notches to be re-treated with H1.2 end-seal.

Wall Cladding

Masterspec 2640 — Weatherboard Cladding
Complete▶
Part 1 — General

Bevel-back weatherboard cladding system to E2/AS1 4th edition. Cavity system with drained and ventilated cavity. Risk matrix assessment per E2/AS1 Table 2 — current score: 4 (Low Risk). Direct-fixed option NOT permitted for this building height/wind zone combination.

Part 2 — Products

180×19mm bevel-back radiata pine weatherboards, H3.1 treated. Cavity battens 20×45mm H3.2 treated at max 600mm centres. Building wrap to BRANZ-appraised product (e.g., Pro Clima Solitex). Flashings: Aluminium 0.7mm, or COLORSTEEL® to corners, windows, and penetrations.

Part 3 — Execution

Fix building wrap over framing, tape all joints min 50mm overlap. Fix cavity battens vertically. Install weatherboards bottom up, min 35mm lap per E2/AS1. Prime all cut ends before fixing. Paint system: 1 coat primer, 2 coats acrylic topcoat. Clearance 175mm min from ground to bottom board.

Metal Roof Cladding

Masterspec 2710 — Metal Roof Cladding
Draft▶
Part 1 — General

Long-run metal roof cladding to E2/AS1. Minimum roof pitch 8° for trapezoidal profile, 3° for standing seam. Roof framing to NZS 3604 Chapter 11. Wind zone: Medium (Auckland suburban). Corrosion zone: B (>1km from coast).

Part 2 — Products

COLORSTEEL® Endura or equivalent, 0.40mm BMT minimum. Trapezoidal profile (Corrugate or equivalent). Flashing material to match roof. Fasteners: Type 17 self-drilling TEK screws with EPDM washers. Sarking: Thermakraft 421 or equivalent.

Part 3 — Execution

Lay sheets from eaves to ridge, fix to purlins at every rib. Side laps: 1 corrugation min. Apply foam infill at ridges and gutters. Seal all penetrations with Dektite or equivalent. Gutter falls min 1:500. Spouting: COLORSTEEL® quarter-round 150mm.
