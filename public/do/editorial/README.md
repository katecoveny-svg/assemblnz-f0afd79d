# DO editorial artwork

`work-in-your-pocket.webp` is original generated concept artwork, created 30 September 2026 for Assembl DO, then resized/encoded to WebP. It is not a device screenshot or evidence of a connected account.

Art direction: tactile blank paperwork, translucent vellum envelope and blank unbranded smartphone framed by a deep-plum lacquer arch; paper/rose palette, sculptural daylight, generous negative space. No text, logo, people, robots or generic AI orb. Uses the canonical DO mark separately in the interface.

The UI uses a still image, a restrained hover transform and reduced-motion fallback. No autoplay video download is required on a phone.
