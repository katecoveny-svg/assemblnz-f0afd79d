# assembl — brand canon

**Source of truth. Last updated 29 July 2026.**
This file supersedes CANON-LOCKED-2026-06-23 (which specified Lato) and every
earlier brand note. If another document disagrees with this one, this one wins.

Written to be read by people *and* by AI agents. An agent asked to make anything
for assembl — a page, a post, a deck, a demo — should be able to follow this file
alone and produce something on brand.

---

## 1. The name

Always **assembl** — lowercase, no full stop, no capital A, even at the start of
a sentence. Never Assemble, Assembl., ASSEMBL, assembly, assembler.
"assemble" as an ordinary verb is fine; the brand is always **assembl**.

Founder: **Kate Hudson**. (Never "Kate Harland" — that is a different name that
appears in some file metadata.)
Company: assembl NZ Limited · NZBN 9429053514950 · Aotearoa New Zealand.

## 2. The locked phrase

> **assembl — intuitive agentic customer journeys**

Use "agentic customer journeys" and "agentic CX" in all material — website,
decks, demos, social, email footers. This phrasing is locked and must appear on
every surface. Homepage line: *"Agentic customer journeys, assembled."*

## 3. What assembl is

Specialist AI agents prepare every step of a customer relationship — first
enquiry to tenth year — and **a named person approves before anything reaches a
customer**. Not a chatbot builder, not workflow automation, not a marketplace.

**The flagship idea — the monetised wait state.** Every agentic journey has a
pause while agents research, draft and check. Today that pause is a spinner.
assembl makes it the most generous moment in the journey: the work shown as it
happens, value earned for the minutes, and one optional question answered back
the other way.

Three tiers: **01** the assembling loader (live) · **02** the sponsored chat line
(a labelled co-pilot) · **03** the joint-venture UI (a shared partner surface).

## 4. Type

| Role | Face | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Body, UI, headlines | **Jost** | 200 / 300 / 400 / 500 / 700 |
| Editorial display, wordmark | Cormorant Garamond | 300 / 400 / 500 |
| Micro-labels, counters, kickers | IBM Plex Mono *or* Space Mono | 300 / 400 / 500 |

Jost replaced Lato site-wide on 28 July 2026 (Kate: *"i want nord as the font on
the site"* — NORD is a brand in her reference pin, not a licensable typeface;
Jost is the Futura-derived geometric she chose from four candidates).
**Do not reintroduce Lato.** Inter Tight survives only inside legacy components.

Headline treatment: uppercase, weight 700, tight leading (1.06–1.2), letter
spacing ~0.01em. Hierarchy comes from **weight and size, never from grey text** —
secondary text is full-ink at lower opacity only where a scrim demands it.

## 6. The mark

An **orbit around a gold core**: two champagne rings passing behind the core at
the top and in front at the bottom, a gold sphere at centre. It means what the
product means — the journey circling, value at the centre.

## 7. 3D and motion

Every 3D object must **mean something** — never decoration.
Vocabulary: hairline chrome and brass threads (tube radius ≤0.03), torus knots,
navy-gloss rounded forms, brass rings, a gold seed/core, restrained gold dust.
Materials: chrome, brushed brass, piano-black gloss, frosted glass, matte
ceramic. Lighting: the softbox recipe (emissive panels in a PMREM environment —
directional lights alone bake to nothing).

Motion explains the system: assembling, connecting, docking, testing, passing
work, requesting approval, completing. Slow enough to read, interruptible,
reduced-motion respected. Never continuous distraction.

**Rules learned the hard way:** never fade the 3D canvas to make text readable
(use a scrim or text-shadow); gate every render loop on visibility; guard
`webglcontextlost`; two live WebGL contexts on one page is how a context gets
dropped.

## 8. Voice

Plain, direct, warm, professional — how a busy NZ operator actually talks.

- **Lead with the person and the work**, not the technology.
- **Be concrete**: the real wait, the real hour, the real form. If a sentence
  could sit on any SaaS site, rewrite it.
- **Define any term the first time it appears.**
- One idea per sentence. Cut throat-clearing.
- "AI" is allowed where it is the clearest word. Never hide that drafting is
  AI-assisted.

**Banned words**: quietly · seamless · unlock · empower · elevate · supercharge ·
cutting-edge · "the work that matters" · "quiet intelligence" · revolutionise ·
game-changing · 10x.

**NZ English**, macrons mandatory and correct (Aotearoa, whānau, mahi, tikanga,
Pīkau, Tōro). Te reo where it earns its place — never decoration, never
ceremonial or sacred text (assembl does not generate karakia, whaikōrero,
mihimihi, pepeha or waiata).

Tagline in use: **"Mahi that earns its proof."**

## 9. The evidence discipline — the one that matters most

**Every number carries its source, or it does not appear.**

No modelled projections, no borrowed slide statistics, no invented percentages.
Where assembl states a fact about another business, it comes from that
business's own published material with the source named. Where assembl has no
data, it says so ("we measure it, or we don't claim it").

**The concept demos are independent concepts, not customers.** Tower, NZ Post,
Southern Cross and the rest have not engaged assembl. Never imply a client
relationship, partnership or endorsement.

**No real company names as sponsors on assembl's own surfaces.** Inside a
buyer's own concept demo their name is correct; on assembl.co.nz, partner slots
are labelled placeholders.

## 10. Product truths that must stay true in copy

- Nothing sends, books, files or commits without a **named person approving it**.
- Agents read only the sources they are given, and say so when they cannot answer.
- Every agent action leaves a **readable record** — plain words, kept.
- assembling never reads prompts, content, files or code — it sees that a wait
  is happening, never what the wait is about.
- Simulated or illustrative screens are always **labelled as such**.
