No-shows and chased deposits quietly cost small operators more than almost anything on their P&L. Nobody builds the fix, because the fix looked expensive.

The fix is a booking form that takes a deposit at the moment someone books. Everyone knows that. Almost nobody built one, because it meant a developer, a payments integration, and a bill that felt bigger than the problem.

Build one thing, week six — and the last of the winter series.

You tell SPARK: "Build me a booking form for my mobile business. Collect the client's details and what they want, let them pick a slot, and take a deposit through Stripe to confirm it. Make it accessible and Privacy Act compliant."

SPARK builds it — a working booking-and-deposit form, in seconds. And because SPARK does Stripe NZ payments and builds to Privacy Act 2020 and WCAG 2.1 AA, the deposit actually gets taken and the client data is handled properly, out of the box.

Now a booking is confirmed by a deposit, not a promise. The no-show rate drops because there's skin in the game. And the diary fills without a single "just chasing that deposit, sorry!" text.

It's yours, end to end. SPARK builds the form; you set the deposit, the slots and the terms, and you run your own diary. It removes the friction — it doesn't take over your business.

That's six tools off the list this winter. The point was never the tools. It was this: the thing you've been meaning to build has been buildable all along.
