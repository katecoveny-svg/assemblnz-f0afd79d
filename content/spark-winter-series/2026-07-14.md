# Episode 2 — Tuesday 14 July 2026
**Tool: The tradie quote calculator** · proves *business calculator tools*

## LinkedIn (9am NZT)

A painter I know quotes every job the same way: standing in the ute, doing the maths on the back of a hand.

Room sizes in his head. Two coats or three. Prep time — always the bit that gets underquoted. Paint, at trade price, plus the bit he adds for the drive. GST on top. Then a number he half-guesses and hopes is right.

He's good at painting. He's not paid to be good at spreadsheets, and building a proper quote tool always meant a developer and a bill he didn't fancy. So it stayed a back-of-the-hand job. For eleven years.

Here's what that costs: not the odd underquote — the inconsistency. Same job, two different days, two different numbers. Quotes that take an evening instead of a smoko break. And the slow leak of margin nobody ever sees, because it's hidden in the guessing.

This is the first "build one thing" of the winter. Watch what it takes now.

You tell SPARK: "Build me a quote calculator for my painting business. Inputs: room dimensions, number of coats, surface condition, paint grade. Work out paint quantity, prep hours, labour at my hourly rate, a travel margin, and add GST. Give me a clean total I can send the client."

SPARK builds it. In seconds. No code, no developer, no $5K invoice.

Now every quote is the same maths, every time — off the phone, on site, in the two minutes before he drives off. Consistent. Costed. GST sorted.

And it's his. He set the rates, he set the margins, he checks the number before it goes out. SPARK built the tool; the pricing calls are still his, exactly where they should be.

That's the whole idea of this series: one real tool, off the list, this week.
