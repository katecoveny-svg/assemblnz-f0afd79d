# Episode 4 — Tuesday 28 July 2026
**Tool: The Healthy Homes compliance checklist** · proves *compliance checklist apps*

## LinkedIn (9am NZT)

Winter is when Healthy Homes stops being paperwork and starts being real.

Heating that has to actually warm the living room on a Dunedin night. Ventilation. Moisture. Draught-stopping. Every rental in the country has to meet the standards, and the landlord with three properties has the exact same obligations as the corporate with three hundred — minus the compliance team.

So the small landlord does it off memory, or off a PDF they half-read, and hopes they haven't missed a standard. Building a proper checklist tool meant a developer. So it never got built.

Build one thing, week four.

You tell SPARK: "Build me a Healthy Homes compliance checklist app. One property per assessment, cover all five standards — heating, insulation, ventilation, moisture and drainage, draught-stopping — with a pass/fail and a notes field for each, and a summary I can keep on file."

SPARK builds it. A working, structured checklist — in seconds, no developer, no five-figure bill.

Now each property gets walked the same way, against every standard, with a record you can actually keep. No more "did I check the extractor fan on that one?" It's the difference between hoping you're compliant and knowing.

And it stays yours. SPARK builds the checklist; the assessment of each property is still yours to make and to sign off. It organises the obligation — it doesn't replace your judgement or your responsibility for the home.

Because meeting the standard shouldn't depend on whether you can afford a back office. It should just be doable.

What's the compliance job you do off memory every winter? Build the checklist.
