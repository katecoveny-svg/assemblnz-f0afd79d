## LinkedIn (9am NZT)

Most small operators don't know their real number until the accountant tells them. By then it's history.

How much of this month's income is actually GST I'm holding for IRD, not money I get to keep? What's left after the bills that haven't hit the account yet? Can I afford that bit of gear before the 28th?

The maths isn't hard. It's just fiddly, and it changes every week, so it lives in a spreadsheet that's always slightly out of date — or in a vague feeling in the back of your mind. Building a live cashflow tool meant a developer, so most people run the business on the feeling.

Build one thing, week five.

You tell SPARK: "Build me a GST and cashflow calculator. Take my income and expenses, set aside the GST portion I owe, show me what's genuinely mine to spend, and let me pull my figures through from Xero."

SPARK builds it — a working calculator, in seconds, no developer bill. And because SPARK does NZ integrations including Xero and IRD, it can draw on the numbers you already have rather than making you key them in twice.

Now the real number is in front of you, not three weeks behind you. The GST you're holding is set aside on paper before you accidentally spend it. And the "can I afford this" question has an actual answer.

It's your tool and your call. SPARK builds the calculator; the financial decisions stay firmly with you and your accountant. It shows you the number sooner — it doesn't make the call for you.

Knowing your real number in real time is one of the biggest advantages big businesses have. This is that advantage, in your pocket.
