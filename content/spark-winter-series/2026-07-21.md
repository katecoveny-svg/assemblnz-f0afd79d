# Episode 3 — Tuesday 21 July 2026
**Tool: The client intake form (+ Privacy Act 2020)** · proves *intake forms + Privacy Act 2020*

## LinkedIn (9am NZT)

The messiest part of most service businesses isn't the work. It's the first ten minutes of a new client.

The back-and-forth emails to get their details. The bits you forget to ask until job three. The client info sitting in a Gmail thread, a text, and a sticky note — three places, none of them safe, all of them a Privacy Act problem waiting to happen.

Everyone means to build a proper intake form. Almost nobody does, because it meant a developer, a form builder subscription, and figuring out the compliance bit yourself.

Build one thing, week three.

You tell SPARK: "Build a client intake form for my consultancy. Collect name, business, contact, the service they want, budget range, and how they found us. Make it Privacy Act 2020 compliant, tell people why we're collecting it, and make it accessible."

SPARK builds it — a clean, working intake form, in seconds. And because SPARK is grounded in the Privacy Act 2020 (including the IPP 3A collection-notice rules) and builds to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility, the compliance groundwork is baked in, not bolted on after.

One link. Every new client answers the same questions, once. The details land in one place instead of three. And the "why are we collecting this" notice — the bit most small operators don't know they legally need — is already there.

It's yours to control. You decide what you ask, you own the data, you check it does what you need before a single client sees it. SPARK builds the form; the judgement about your clients stays with you.

The professional front door your business deserved, without the developer bill.
