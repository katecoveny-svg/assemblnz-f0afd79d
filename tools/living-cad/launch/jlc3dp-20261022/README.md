## Notes for the quote page

- MJF nylon is solid and strong but one colour; the sun yellow and cream are for later prints. The ruler lids and pod still
  test fit and scale in black; the two-colour inlays (not in this zip) need a two-colour FDM print later.
- The keys test the TURN clamp; MJF nylon is a little softer than PETG, so the clamp result is a lower bound.
- Before you pay, compare: Print a Part NZ$622.55 incl GST; 3D Print It (reply due by about 21:55 NZT on 22 October).
- Estimate before the quote: about NZ$250–330 incl DHL and GST (US$0.275/g MJF, PA12-CF from US$3 a part).
