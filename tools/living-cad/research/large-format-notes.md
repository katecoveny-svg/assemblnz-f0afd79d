Large-format research notes (subagent report, 19 Oct 2026) — key findings kept for the guide
- A2L: 330x320x325 mm, open frame, PLA/PETG/TPU class; up to 19 colours (4 AMS + AMS Lite); announced June 2026; PB Tech NZ$799 standalone. Sources: all3dp.com A2L article; 3dprintingindustry.com A2L specs; pbtech.co.nz PTRBAM0034
- H2D 325x320x325 (heated chamber 65C); H2S 340x320x340; X1C/P1S/P2S 256 cube
- Normal farm envelope ~256 mm; design every part to 250 mm cube; furniture = connectors + timber
- Chois dowel connector system (Yanko Design Sept 2026) proves the market for printed joints + dowels
- Lampshades: PLA deformed over incandescent in 2-3 h, fine with LED (facfox); keep 20 mm gap; PETG or better; sell shade only. NZ: light fittings/portable luminaires are declared articles (WorkSafe medium/high-risk lists); bundling cord set makes her the supplier -> ask Energy Safety
- Fishing: printed rod blanks are novelty; make racks, holders, tackle trays, handline winders, grips for bought blanks; no hooks in teen kits
- 3DAPI: no published max part size; AU/NZ US$9.49 <=600 g; prefers Bambu 3MF
- No NZ/AU farm found advertising large-format drop-ship
