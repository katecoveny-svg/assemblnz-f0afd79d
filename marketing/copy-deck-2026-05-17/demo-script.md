# Demo Script

This is Assembl.

Assembl is built for the work that cannot just be automated — it has to be
trusted.

We help New Zealand organisations run operational workflows through specialist
agents, with human review and evidence packs built in.

So instead of an AI output appearing as a black box, every workflow carries
its working:

- what sources were used,
- what rules were checked,
- what assumptions were made,
- who reviewed it,
- and what decision or action came next.

We call this mahi that earns its proof.

The starting point is simple: bring us one workflow.

It might be an RFI process, a compliance check, a customer response workflow,
a school notice process, a freight exception, a policy summary, or an
internal reporting task.

In a 10-working-day pilot sprint, we map the workflow, design the agent
process, add review points, and produce a working evidence pack.

The goal is not to replace your people.

The goal is to give them time back, reduce operational drag, and make
AI-assisted work easier to trust.

Assembl. Specialist agents. Human review. Evidence packs. Built in Aotearoa.
