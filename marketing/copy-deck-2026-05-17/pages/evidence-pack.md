## Why this matters

- **Eyebrow:** Why this matters
- **Heading:** Audit-ready by design.
- **Body:** Work is only useful when someone can trust how it was made.
  Assembl treats the evidence pack as the finish line for every specialist
  agent run: what came in, which rule was checked, who reviewed the draft,
  what changed, and when the work was sealed. The pack gives operators a
  calm way to show their reasoning without reconstructing a week from
  email threads and memory.
- **CTA:** Ship one in a Pilot Sprint → `/pilot-sprint`
