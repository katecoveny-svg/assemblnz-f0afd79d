- **For:** teams ready to run multiple workflows through Assembl.

- **Includes:** multiple kete workflows, admin dashboard, reporting, evidence
  library, and human review rules.

## Enterprise / Sector Partner

- **For:** organisations, industry bodies, or government-linked programmes.
- **Investment:** custom pricing
- **Includes:** governance design, custom agent packs, sector-level reporting,
  integrations, training, and support.
