## Proof section

- **Heading:** Every output carries its working.
- **Copy:** Assembl is built for the work that needs a trail. Each agent
  workflow is configured with rules, review points, source checks, assumptions,
  and evidence outputs so teams can see what happened, who reviewed it, and
  why it can be trusted.

### Three proof cards

1. **Grounded in NZ context** — Workflows are shaped around New Zealand
   operating conditions, relevant laws, sector expectations, and the policies
   your team already works within.
2. **Reviewed by a named person** — Agents can draft, check, compare, classify,
   and summarise — but sensitive outputs are reviewed by an accountable human
   before they are used.
3. **Sealed with an evidence pack** — Each workflow produces a traceable pack:
   sources, decisions, assumptions, checks, review notes, timestamps, and
   next actions.
