# Pilot Sprint

- **Headline:** Start with one workflow. Leave with proof.
- **Subhead:** A focused 10-working-day sprint to turn one operational process
  into an agent-assisted workflow with human review and an evidence pack.
- **Body:** Bring us one repeatable workflow — an RFI process, school notice
  workflow, compliance check, onboarding task, customer response, reporting
  pack, logistics process, or internal review. We'll map it, design the agent
  workflow, define the human review points, and produce a working proof demo.

## What's included

- Discovery and workflow mapping
- Agent workflow design
- Policy, rule, and system setup
- Human review configuration
- Evidence pack design
- Pilot run and refinement
- Handover and adoption plan

## Specs

- **Timeline:** 10 working days
- **Investment:** From $18,000 + GST
- **Deliverable:** A live pilot workflow with evidence pack and adoption plan
- **CTA:** Book a pilot
