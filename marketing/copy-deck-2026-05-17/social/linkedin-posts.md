# LinkedIn — 20 Post Starters

1. **Proof, not just automation.** AI can draft fast. But serious work needs
   more than speed — it needs context, review, and a trail.

2. **Agents do the mahi. People own the outcome.** That is the operating
   principle behind Assembl.

3. **The next AI advantage in New Zealand won't be chatbots.** It will be
   operational workflows that teams can actually trust.

4. **What is an evidence pack?** A simple idea: every AI-assisted output
   should show its sources, assumptions, checks, review notes, and decision
   path.

5. **Built in Aotearoa.** Not because geography is branding — because context
   matters.

6. **Compliance is not the product. Trust is.** Compliance is one part of the
   trust layer.

7. **AI adoption is stuck when teams cannot prove the work.** Assembl is
   designed for the gap between "the AI said it" and "we can stand behind it."

8. **The most useful agents will be boring in the best way.** They will chase
   missing info, prepare packs, check rules, draft summaries, and keep work
   moving.

9. **One workflow is enough to start.** Don't transform the whole business.
   Pick one painful process and prove it.

10. **Human review is not a weakness.** It is how agentic systems become
    usable in real organisations.

11. **For construction:** Imagine RFIs, QA checks, safety notes, and programme
    risks moving through one proof-backed workflow.

12. **For hospitality:** Food safety, incident notes, staff onboarding, event
    readiness — all with a trail.

13. **For logistics:** Freight exceptions, customs documents, customer
    updates, and carrier comparisons need speed and traceability.

14. **For education:** Newsletters, dates, gear lists, parent updates, and
    school logistics should not live in inbox chaos.

15. **For government:** AI-assisted work needs transparency by design, not as
    an afterthought.

16. **The question is changing.** Not "can AI do this?" but "can we prove how
    the work was done?"

17. **Assembl's thesis:** Agentic AI becomes valuable when it is
    simulation-tested, policy-governed, and human-reviewed.

18. **The pilot sprint offer:** Give us one operational workflow. We'll turn
    it into proof in 10 working days.

19. **Why vessels?** Because Assembl holds work, review, context, and
    evidence together.

20. **Mahi that earns its proof.** That is the bar.
