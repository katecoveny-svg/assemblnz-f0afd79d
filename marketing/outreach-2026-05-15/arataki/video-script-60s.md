## Direction notes

- **The "dispute tribunal will never have to guess" line is the closer.** MVDT disputes are the operator's worst nightmare. Showing that assembl produces evidence the tribunal would accept BEFORE a dispute happens is the unfair advantage.
- **Voice tone:** straightforward, no-drama. Workshop people respect plain talk over polish.
- **Title card (1.5s start):** "assembl · arataki" in Cormorant Garamond.
- **End card (last 2s):** assembl wordmark + `assembl.co.nz`.
- **Subtitle the whole video.**
- **Important framing:** assembl does NOT submit MR13B (ownership change). Buyer + seller do that themselves. The video doesn't need to belabour this but make sure it doesn't accidentally imply the agent registers vehicles. Per Plugin Architecture Canon §8.1, vehicle registration is never-build MCP.
