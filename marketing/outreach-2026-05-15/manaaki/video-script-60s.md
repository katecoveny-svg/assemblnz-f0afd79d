## Script (read while screen-recording)

### 0:00 – 0:05 — Hook
> **"Watch a Manaaki agent prep an entire quarter of food-safety records for a verifier visit."**

**Show:** `app.assembl.co.nz/manaaki/inbox` — verification pack at top of inbox

### 0:05 – 0:15 — The problem
> **"Verifier visit on Friday. Three months of receiving logs, calibrations, allergen records, cleaning checks. Café owner usually loses a Sunday to this. We do it Tuesday lunchtime."**

**Show:** Hover the verification pack row → preview pane opens → calendar widget shows "Verifier visit · Fri 4 Apr"

### 0:15 – 0:45 — Live walkthrough

**0:15 – 0:25** (10s)
> **"Agent pulled 89 days of trading. Cross-referenced each log against the Food Control Plan. Found two gaps. Surfaced them — not hidden, not auto-fixed."**

**Show:** Open pack → scroll to Reasoning Trace → highlight "Two logging gaps identified" → click to expand

**0:25 – 0:35** (10s)
> **"Gap one — bain-marie thermometer calibration missed on the 14th of February. Gap two — open supplier item from the 22nd of March, poultry delivery temperature was six degrees. Both visible to the verifier."**

**Show:** Two gap entries → each one expanded → corrective-action language drafted but unapproved

**0:35 – 0:45** (10s)
> **"Operator accepts the corrective actions. Adds a note about the supplier follow-up. Signs. The pack seals."**

**Show:** Click first gap → "Accept" → second gap → type a note "Spoke to supplier yesterday, new delivery 27 Mar" → "Accept" → "Sign and seal pack" button

### 0:45 – 0:55 — The output zoom

> **"This is what the verifier sees on Friday. Q1 logs. Gaps disclosed. Corrective actions signed by the owner. Citations to the Food Act and MPI Template FCP. No surprises in the visit."**

**Show:** Click "View pack PDF" → PDF opens → scroll through: cover → citations → reviewer record signed → hash chain

### 0:55 – 1:00 — CTA

> **"Mahi that earns its proof. Try it: assembl.co.nz."**

**Show:** Switch tab to `assembl.co.nz/kete/manaaki` — new homepage hero visible

## Direction notes

- **Voice tone:** practical, calm, hospitality-friendly. Avoid technical jargon. Verifier visits make café owners nervous — assembl is the calming force, not another anxiety vector.
- **Title card (1.5s start):** "assembl · manaaki" in Cormorant Garamond, against warm cream background.
- **End card (last 2s):** assembl wordmark + `assembl.co.nz`.
- **Subtitle the whole video.**
- **The two-gap moment is the punchline.** Surfacing gaps + giving the operator language to disclose them is the differentiator vs every other "AI for food safety" pitch. Lean into it.
