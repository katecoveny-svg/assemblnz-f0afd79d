## Direction notes

- **The "agent does not lodge" line is the single most important sentence.** Licensed brokers are nervous about AI doing customs work for legal-liability reasons. Hammer it: agent drafts, broker lodges. Three times if you can.
- **Voice tone:** precise, professional. Brokers respect competence. Avoid hype.
- **Title card (1.5s start):** "assembl · pīkau" in Cormorant Garamond. Note the macron.
- **End card (last 2s):** assembl wordmark + `assembl.co.nz`.
- **Subtitle the whole video.**
- **For Aironaut pilot specifically:** Kate's dad is the licensed broker. He'll watch this video as the operator. Frame the broker-not-agent boundary clearly so he can show it to other brokers in CBAFF without legal anxiety.
