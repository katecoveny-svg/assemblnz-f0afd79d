# Tax Navigator — IRD

## Problem

Working for Families and small-business tax positions change faster than many citizens can report them. IRD carries the downstream rework; people carry the confusion. The opportunity is to catch changes early, explain them plainly, and prepare the right draft for review.

## The Agent

**Tax Navigator · Tauira Tāke** scans approved income and context signals, then drafts IRD-facing or accountant-facing explanations, payment-plan notes, and source-backed next steps.

## Workflow

- Kahu masks taxpayer identifiers before retrieval or model use.
- PCO-backed retrieval cites tax and privacy legislation where relevant.
- The agent drafts a tax-position summary, risk flags, and plain-English explanation.
- An IRD officer or authorised accountant reviews and signs before anything is sent.

## Assurance Posture

- Draft-only; no binding tax advice and no automatic filing.
- Named reviewer required on every output.
- Evidence pack preserves source citations, assumptions, and reviewer decisions.
- Cross-border model use is disclosed and controlled by workflow risk.
- Tenant isolation and audit logging are required before any agency pilot.

## Procurement Path

The practical path is a small pilot through IRD Te Pou or an mGCDO Innovation Marketplace sandbox, then AoG ICT Services panel eligibility once security and privacy gates are mature.

## Gate

Public Assembly remains pre-incorporation. No agency-facing promise should be made until the governance, iwi, privacy, and security gates are complete.
