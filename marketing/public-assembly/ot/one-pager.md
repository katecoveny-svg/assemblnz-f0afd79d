# Case Navigator — Oranga Tamariki

## Problem

Frontline social workers spend a large share of their time on case documentation. Record quality matters deeply: for whānau, for courts, for accountability, and for the long tail of decisions people may need to understand years later.

## The Agent

**Case Navigator · Whanaungatanga** prepares draft case-record entries, whānau handoff notes, and Family Group Conference brief material from approved inputs, always queued for a named worker and iwi-sponsored reviewer.

## Workflow

- Kahu masks personal information before retrieval or model use.
- PCO-backed retrieval cites Oranga Tamariki, children, evidence, and privacy legislation where relevant.
- The agent drafts structured notes, safety flags, and handoff summaries.
- A named worker and iwi-sponsored review path approve before sealing.

## Assurance Posture

- Highest care category; no auto-send and no care recommendation.
- Iwi sponsorship is non-negotiable.
- Named human sign-off required on every output.
- Evidence pack preserves citations, assumptions, reviewer notes, and timestamps.
- MEADS-alignment and agency-specific PIA must be completed before pilot use.

## Procurement Path

The appropriate route is not a cold software sale. It starts with iwi-led sponsorship, then an Oranga Tamariki Digital Practice Tools conversation, and only then a tightly scoped sandbox pilot.

## Gate

Do not promote this as a live product. It is a gated preview until iwi sponsorship, Māori-elder review, cultural safety review, and agency privacy work are complete.
