# Entitlement Navigator — MSD

## Problem

Beneficiaries miss entitlements while frontline MSD officers spend too much of the day on form-completion and eligibility checking. The work that needs human judgement — noticing context, explaining options, and deciding what should happen next — is squeezed by administrative rework.

## The Agent

**Entitlement Navigator · Manaakitanga** continuously scans a beneficiary record for entitlements not currently claimed, then drafts the application or case note for an MSD case manager to approve and sign.

## Workflow

- Kahu masks personal information before retrieval or model use.
- PCO-backed retrieval cites the Social Security Act 2018 and Privacy Act 2020 where relevant.
- The agent drafts candidate entitlement checks, reasoning notes, and application language.
- The case manager edits, approves, or rejects. Output stays `pending_approval` until signed.

## Assurance Posture

- Draft-only; no auto-send and no binding entitlement decision.
- Named human reviewer required before sealing.
- Evidence pack records inputs, citations, assumptions, reviewer notes, and timestamps.
- Privacy Act 2020 and IPP 3A obligations surfaced in the workflow.
- Iwi advisory and MEADS-alignment gate required before whānau-sensitive deployment.

## Procurement Path

The wedge is an mGCDO Innovation Marketplace sandbox with MSD Digital Channels as an anchor host, followed by AoG ICT Services panel work if the pilot proves value. Public Assembly remains pre-incorporation; the evidence pack is the pitch, not a claim of production readiness.

## Gate

Iwi sponsorship and the §8 Māori-elder conversation must happen before any public-sector whānau deployment.
