# Study Navigator — MoE / StudyLink

## Problem

StudyLink eligibility, course costs, parental-income changes, accommodation support, and ECE funding declarations are high-friction workflows. Students and providers need clear next steps; staff need fewer repeated explanations and cleaner evidence trails.

## The Agent

**Study Navigator · Ako** turns student-support or ECE funding rules into plain-language draft guidance, evidence requests, and reviewer-ready checklists.

## Workflow

- Kahu masks student or centre identifiers before retrieval or model use.
- PCO-backed retrieval cites the Education and Training Act 2020, student-support rules, and Privacy Act 2020 where relevant.
- The agent drafts pathway options, missing-evidence requests, and risk flags.
- A student-support officer or funding reviewer approves before any communication leaves.

## Assurance Posture

- Draft-only; no approval or decline decision.
- Named human reviewer required.
- Evidence pack records source material, draft rationale, and sign-off status.
- Youth/student data is treated as sensitive by default.
- Te Tiriti and Māori data considerations must be reviewed before public-sector deployment.

## Procurement Path

The wedge is MoE Digital Services for Students or an mGCDO Innovation Marketplace pilot. ECE funding use cases can follow through the Education System Lift programme once evidence and privacy controls are proven.

## Gate

This is pilot-ready positioning only. Public Assembly is not production-promised until incorporation, iwi review, and agency-specific PIA work are complete.
