# Assembl Living AL-05 prototype

A complete **local** engineering handoff was generated for AL-05 with 8 CadQuery STEP/STL parts, executable generator, dimensions and mesh checks. The CAD ZIP is delivered separately in the associated conversation; binary CAD assets are **not yet hosted in this repository**.

## Status
- CAD: generated and digital mesh closure checked; physical validation pending.
- Two fluted cups and desktop dock: shallow locating recesses, **not positively locked**.
- LINK-05 male and three female dovetail specimens: fit coupons only, **not a validated latch**.
- GRID-05 tile: experimental panel, **no wall fixing or load rating**.
- Checkout and printer dispatch: remain disabled.

## Next acceptance gate
Print test coupons in PLA and PETG, measure clearances, revise geometry, perform retention tests, then build adapter variants on a common mechanical interface. Publish versioned CAD-derived viewer meshes and product imagery from the approved solids. Do not claim verified production or IP exclusivity.
