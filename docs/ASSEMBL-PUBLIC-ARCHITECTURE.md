# assembl public architecture

**Status:** current public-facing source of truth  
**Updated:** 17 September 2026

## Company

**assembl the work.**

**find it. DO it. show it.**

assembl is one connected system for turning signals into useful work and useful work into visible proof.

## Products

### Pursuit — find it.

Turns relevant change into an evidence-backed opportunity.

### DO — DO it.

Moves bounded work forward with the right agent, context, tools and permissions.

### Studio — show it.

Turns an opportunity or completed work into something people can see, test, approve or buy.

## What is not a top-level product

The following are capabilities, specialist surfaces or supporting systems beneath Pursuit / DO / Studio:

- specialist agents;
- agentic customer journeys;
- useful / rewarded / sponsored waits;
- loyalty and partner value;
- Business Genome;
- evidence receipts;
- meetings;
- household tools;
- tender workflows;
- industry packs;
- ad / pattern / motion / concept studios;
- browser and desktop companions;
- model routing;
- connectors and integrations.

Do not promote one of these into a peer public brand unless the company architecture is deliberately changed.

## Copy rules

Prefer:

- concrete verbs;
- short declarative sentences;
- customer-visible outcomes;
- visible truth labels;
- clear approval boundaries.

Avoid exposing internal implementation notes such as:

- build-order comments;
- component names;
- “canvas first / radar behind the scenes”;
- “surface is not the agent” as public headline copy;
- asset reuse notes;
- repository or deployment language.

## Truth boundary

Preview, proposed, simulated and live states must remain distinct.

A connected key, working UI, successful build or polished demonstration is not proof that an external action happened.

Consequential actions such as sending, publishing, spending or irreversible changes require the configured authority and should leave evidence.
