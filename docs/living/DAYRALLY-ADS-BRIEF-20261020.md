# dayrally — ads brief for Meta (Instagram, Facebook) and TikTok

**Status, 20 October 2026:** every product image is a render from the real print files; nothing has been
printed; every store product is a draft. This brief is written so the first creative can be made now
(phase 0, labelled concept) and re-cut the day the first prints pass. It is the hand-off for whoever makes
the ads: Meta's creative tools, an agency, a creator or us in the Remotion pipeline.

Canon this brief draws on: `DESIGN-BRIEF-20261019.md` §11.6–11.10 (the boards), `DAYRALLY-RALLY-SERIES.md`
(the mascot and the series rules), `docs/assembl-brand-system.md` (the scoped Fredoka and sun exceptions).
Where this brief and those disagree, those win.

## 1. The one-screen brand

- **Name and wordmark.** `dayrally`, lowercase, Fredoka 700, always followed by the small rounded-rectangle
  key glyph. Sun on white, white on sun, or black on white. Never stretched, never outlined, never in
  another face.
- **Line.** one system. endless days. (tomato, lowercase, full stops.)
- **Description.** Modular 3D printed accessories that click together and go wherever you do.
- **The idea in four words.** click. lock. change. go. One key fits every mount: the same push-and-quarter-
  turn key locks a pod to a bike, a bag strap, a desk dock, a helmet, a collar.
- **Palette.** Sun `#FFD200` on screen (`#F5C400` is the printed PETG sun), tomato `#E8472B`, black `#1A1A1A`, cream `#EFE6D6`, white. Four colours on a
  board, never more than three on one object. One hero colour and one accent per piece of creative.
- **Type.** Fredoka 700 for the wordmark and short display lines only. Instrument Sans for everything else.
  Captions in the hand-lettered style: black marker, two or three words a line, a sun underline.
- **Activities.** ride · explore · everyday · pet · camp · fish.
- **Kits and their lines.** ride kit: mount it. move it. go further. · everyday kit: clip it. organise it.
  make it yours. · explore kit: take good things further. · components library: Mix, match and make it
  yours. All 3D printed. Designed for real days.
- **Mascot.** rally, a long-haired dachshund in a white helmet, black sunglasses, a yellow harness with an
  action cam, a sun-yellow bone tag that says RALLY. rally demonstrates and never sells, never speaks, and
  is an illustration until a real dog has worn a real part.
- **Made by.** assembl / living, Auckland. The company brand (deep plum, Instrument Sans, no yellow) never
  appears inside dayrally creative beyond a small "by assembl" in the end card.

## 2. Who we are talking to, and the platform rules that shape it

- **Primary, paid:** 18 to 24 in Aotearoa New Zealand, bikes, trails, beaches, boats, dogs. People who
  personalise their gear and share it. Secondary, paid: parents 30 to 50 buying for 10 to 17 year olds
  (school kit, scoot kit, pet kit).
- **Under 18:** reached organically through the rally series, not paid. Both platforms restrict what can
  be targeted to minors and change the rules often; the campaign targets 18+ and lets the series do the
  rest. Nothing in the creative is designed to pressure a child to buy.
- **Where they are:** Instagram Reels and Explore, TikTok For You, Facebook Reels for the parents. Stories
  for the behind-the-scenes prints.

## 3. Phases: what we can say when

| Phase | When | What the ad can claim | Objective | Where it sends people |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 · concept | now | "concept · not yet printed" on every frame that shows a part | reach, views, follows, waitlist sign-ups | the live site: the lab pages on www.assembl.co.nz/living/widget, a waitlist form |
| 1 · printed | first prints pass | "printed prototype" on real-part footage; renders may stay for the library | traffic, saves, waitlist | the dayrally page on the store once Kate publishes the theme and the page |
| 2 · available | products set active in the store | "available" and a price, no sooner | sales | product pages |

Rule across all phases: never claim a part exists, ships, or is in stock before it does. No "limited drop",
no countdown, no fake scarcity, no fake reviews or unboxings, no "as seen on". Trust is the product.

## 4. Creative rules (from the series bible, binding for ads too)

1. One trick, one part, per piece. click, lock, change, go. Nothing else happens.
2. Vertical 9:16 for Reels and TikTok, 7 to 15 seconds, no intro, the trick in the first second, the
   wordmark and key glyph in the last.
3. One hero colour and one accent, from the sun / tomato / black / cream set.
4. The parts are always our renders or our prints. No AI-art textures on a product. The boards are
   reference for mood only and never appear in an ad.
5. Motion is work happening: the key turns, the lid opens, the slider clicks. Nothing bounces or sparkles.
6. Sound: the click. Then a human voice or trending audio, never a robot voice. Captions on, always.
7. The honesty line in the caption of every piece that shows an unprinted part.
8. rally is the demonstrator in the corner or on the end card; rally never holds a product that has not
   been printed and worn.

## 5. Reels and TikTok: six scripts to make first

Each is 7 to 15 seconds, 1080 × 1920. Shot list, then on-screen caption (hand-lettered style), then the
spoken or text line, then end card. Frames come from the Remotion pipeline on the real STLs (phase 0) or
phone video of the prints (phase 1).

**A · one key.** 0 to 1 s: a cube drops onto the sun-yellow universal key, quarter-turn, click. 1 to 6 s:
pull, it holds; a second cube clicks onto the first; a third. 6 to 9 s: the chain locks onto a bike bar.
Caption: "one key." then "fits everything." End card: wordmark, key glyph, "click. lock. change. go."
Hero colour sun, accent black. Honesty: concept · not yet printed.

**B · same key. more freedom.** The cam mount comes off the bars, clicks onto the helmet base, clicks onto
rally's collar mount. Three clicks, three seconds each. Caption: "bars." "helmet." "collar." Line: "same
key. more freedom." Hero tomato, accent cream.

**C · click. lock. change. go.** The bottle cup lifts off the bike with a quarter-turn and drops onto the
desk dock as a pen cup. Caption: "bike." "desk." Line: "good days are modular." Hero sun, accent black.

**D · mount it. move it.** Phone into the 45° channels of the ride mount, slider clicks down, ride, lift the
tab, phone out. Caption: "in." "ride." "out." Line: "mount it. move it. go further." Hero black, accent sun.

**E · make it yours.** The ride pod opens, the name slate pops off with a thumbnail, a new name in Fredoka
goes on, the lid closes. Caption: "your name." "your kit." Line: "clip it. organise it. make it yours."
Hero cream, accent tomato.

**G · scoot. fish.** (phase 1 onward, once the stem saddle has been printed and strapped to a real scooter) The
stem saddle straps onto a share scooter's stem in one motion, the quiver clicks on with a quarter-turn, the packed
rod drops in, ride to the wharf, lift the rod out, unstrap, dock the scooter. Caption: "strap." "click." "fish."
Line: "take good things further." Hero sun, accent black. Honesty: the Flamingo or Lime is a hired scooter, the
parts come off before it is returned, and the ad never shows a part left on a share scooter.

**F · plan. build. print. go.** Screen capture of the widget: pick ride, pick sun, type RALLY, the parts
assemble in 3D, the part sheet appears. Caption: "plan." "build." "print." "go." This one is honest today
because the widget is live and produces the real print files; it never shows a printer running.

Hooks to A/B on the first frame (text only, 3 to 5 words): "one key. fits everything." · "this is one
key." · "wait for the click." · "same key, three mounts." · "3D printed. clicks on."

## 6. Carousels (Instagram and Facebook, 1080 × 1350 or 1080 × 1080, 5 to 8 cards)

**Carousel 1 · the components library.** Card 1: the sun field, wordmark, "one key. endless possibilities."
Cards 2 to 7: one cube each on cream, name in Fredoka, one line in Instrument Sans: universal key ("the
thing everything clicks onto"), base mount ("bars, poles, flat stuff"), angle joint ("45°, then another
45°"), tube clamp ("22 to 35 mm bars"), device holder ("phone, light, cam"), end cap ("finish the chain").
Card 8: the chain on the bike, "click. lock. change. go." Render source: `mini-library-sun`, `mini-chain-sun`.

**Carousel 2 · the ride kit.** Card 1: `bike-set-sun`, "mount it. move it. go further." Then one card each
for the rack pod, the top-tube saddle, the phone mount, the bottle cup, the number plate, the grips. Last
card: the kit laid out, "ride kit." Render sources: `bike-set-sun`, `bike-toptube-sun`, `ride-phone-ink`,
`ride-bottle-tangerine`, `sun-number-plate-lime`, `ride-grips-lime`.

**Carousel 3 · the everyday kit.** Card 1: `sun-dayrally-everyday-flatlay`, "clip it. organise it. make it
yours." Then pod mini, pod standard, bag charm, zip pulls, strap clip, home dock. Last card: "everyday kit."
Render sources: `sun-pod-mini-butter-name`, `sun-pod-standard-sky-waves`, `sun-charm-star-tangerine`.

**Carousel 5 · the scooter.** Card 1: the stem saddle on a stem, "strap. click. go." Then the stem saddle alone,
the quiver, the packed rod in the quiver, the pod mini on the same saddle, the bar saddle on the handlebar. Last
card: "scoot kit." with the honesty line. Render source: `scoot-set-sun`. Phase 1 onward, since a share scooter
is in the frame and the parts must have been strapped to a real one first.

**Carousel 4 · one key, six days.** Six cards, one activity each in the board's icon style: ride, explore,
everyday, pet, camp, fish. Each card: the activity word in Fredoka, one part that belongs to it, the key
glyph. Last card: "one system. endless days."

Carousel copy rule: the first card carries the hook, the last card carries the wordmark and the honesty line,
every middle card names one part and says one thing it does. No prices in phase 0 or 1.

## 10. Test plan and proof

- Start small: six Reels (A to F) and two carousels (1 and 2), NZD 20 a day per platform for 14 days,
  Advantage+ placements on Meta, Smart Performance off on TikTok so we can read the creative.
- Read after 72 hours and again at day 7: hook rate (3-second views over impressions), completion rate,
  saves and shares, follows, waitlist sign-ups, cost per sign-up. Pause anything under half the best
  performer's hook rate; put its budget on the winner; cut a new hook on the loser's trick.
- What we are learning in phase 0 is which trick people watch, not what they buy. That decides which part
  prints first after the bone tag and which episode of the series goes next.
- Proof lives in the repo: a fortnightly line in `docs/factory/LEARNINGS.md` with the numbers and the call.

## 11. Do not

- Do not use the company plum, the company wordmark, or Fredoka on any assembl company surface.
- Do not show a part as real, worn, shipped or in stock before it is.
- Do not put rally on a real dog's photo, or a real dog in a printed part, until it has happened.
- Do not use AI-generated product imagery; the renders come from the print files or they do not exist.
- Do not use canary yellow anywhere but dayrally; sun `#F5C400` is the dayrally colour and stays there.
- Do not run "Shop now" before products are active.
- Do not target under-18s with paid media.
