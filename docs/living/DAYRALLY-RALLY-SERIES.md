# rally the dishound — the dayrally mascot and the short-video series

**Status: series bible, 20 October 2026. The character is Kate's AI-generated concept art; no episode exists
yet; every product shown is a render of an unprinted part until the first print.** Boards:
`docs/living/boards/dayrally-look-20261020.jpg` and the four sent after it (described in brief 11.9).

## The character

**rally.** A long-haired dachshund (the boards call the breed-joke "the dishound"). White mountain-bike helmet
with the `dayrally` wordmark on the side, black sunglasses, a yellow harness with a yellow action cam and a
red photo tag clipped to it. Always outdoors, always sitting on a rock or a trail with a white bike behind.
Expressions on the boards: squint, grin with tongue, head-tilt, ears up, howl, the peace-paw. Lines the boards
give rally: "good dogs. greater days." · "same key. more freedom." · "good gear. more days." · "same dog. more
days." · "adventure companion. always ready."

rally is **the demonstrator**, never the salesperson. rally wears the kit, loses the kit, swaps the kit, and
the kit survives. rally never speaks; the captions and a human voice do. Caption style from the rally line board:
hand-lettered black marker, two or three words a line, a sun underline. Lines to use as captions and end
cards: "good days go further." · "trails. tails. good days." · "early starts. brighter days." · "same crew.
different horizons." · "on board for more." · "slower moments. bigger days." · "explore together." · "plan.
build. go."

## Places

The later boards put rally in Auckland: the Waitākere trails, the Sky Tower across the Waitematā from a boat,
Muriwai at sunset, a snapper on the deck. Shoot the series there. rally's collar carries the sun-yellow
**bone tag** (`mini-bone-tag-name`, RALLY raised in Fredoka) in every frame; it is the one part that is in
every episode, so it is the first part to print.

## Rules

- Every episode shows one trick with one part: click, lock, change, go. Nothing else happens.
- One hero colour and one accent per episode, from the sun / tomato / black / cream set.
- Vertical, 9:16, 7 to 15 seconds, no intro, the trick in the first second, the wordmark in the last.
- The honesty line is in the caption of every episode that shows an unprinted part: "concept · not yet
  printed" until the part exists; "printed prototype" after; "available" only when the store says so.
- rally is an illustration until a real dog has worn a real part. When that happens, the real dog is the
  star and the illustration becomes the avatar.
- No fake reviews, no fake unboxings, no "limited drop", no AI-art textures on the product itself: the
  parts are always our renders or our prints.

## Production path

1. **Now:** renders from the real STLs (`tools/living-cad/render/`) in the board palette, animated in the
   Remotion pipeline (`remotion/`): the key turning, the lid opening, the slider clicking, each a 2-second
   clip; rally as a still illustration in the corner, or the avatar ring as the profile. The motion rules of
   the brand hold: motion is work happening, nothing bounces.
2. **After the first prints:** phone video of the real parts doing the trick, the illustration retired to the
   avatar.
3. **After a real dog:** rally is a real dog. Collar mount, light, cam. That is the pet kit's proof.

Assets to make first: the avatar ring (helmet, glasses, sun circle) at 1:1 for profile pictures; six
expression stickers from the boards; a 9:16 end card with the wordmark in Fredoka 700 and the key glyph.
