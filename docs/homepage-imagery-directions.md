# assembl homepage imagery directions

The homepage should use one coherent object family rather than a collection of unrelated beautiful images.

## 1. Journey folio

**Placement:** opening hero.  
**Frame:** top-down wide flatlay.  
**Story:** an application envelope, permission tab, checklist, prepared brief, reviewer card and evidence receipt resolve into one complete folio.

## 2. Journey aperture

**Placement:** active wait and phone section.  
**Frame:** thin dusty-rose metal phone centred between inputs and output.  
**Story:** approved pieces enter from the left, the customer makes one choice on the phone, and a review folio exits on the right.

## 3. Evidence macro

**Placement:** system or proof section.  
**Frame:** close crop of vellum layers, a timestamp, source markers, customer edits and named-review tab.  
**Story:** proof is a visible part of the object, not a footer disclaimer.

## 4. Human handoff

**Placement:** pilot section.  
**Frame:** a reviewer’s hands correcting the prepared folio with the role and approval state visible. Avoid identifiable customer data.  
**Story:** assembl prepares. A named person decides.

## 5. Client translation system

**Placement:** client demonstrators.  
**Frame:** keep the camera, spacing and assembly rhythm. Replace the artefacts and one active accent with the client’s real world.  
**Examples:** travel documents for Air New Zealand, a household energy plan for Contact, a family visit brief for Summerset, a service-and-mobility pack for Winger.

## Required exports

- 16:10 hero still at 2400 px wide;
- 9:16 mobile crop;
- six-to-eight-second MP4 and WebM loop;
- poster still matching the first video frame;
- fully assembled reduced-motion still.
