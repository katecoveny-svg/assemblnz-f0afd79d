# Dash — Messaging Brief (copy alignment for design handoff)

**For:** the design/build pass on the `/dash` site.
**Purpose:** keep every word on-brand and consistent. Pair with the locked visual
system (white · yellow · charcoal, Lato) and the strategy report.

---

## 1. What Dash is (one line)
> **Dash is a lightweight SDK that turns the wait inside NZ digital products into a rewarded, brand-safe moment — installed in minutes.**

Say it as a **loader/SDK**, never as an "ad network." "Ad network" sounds big and risky; "the Dashhound loader you install in two lines" sounds practical, testable, fast.

## 2. Positioning leads (in priority order)
1. **AI builders / agentic wait** (hero wedge): *"Reward the time your agent spends working."* The minutes a user waits while an AI agent works are the biggest, most captive wait in software.
2. **Publishers**: *"Turn dead time into yield."* Your loading states are inventory you already own.
3. **Advertisers**: *"Own the moment between click and result."* The cleanest five seconds in digital.
4. **People**: *"Get a little back while you wait."* Opt-in, rewards (not cash).
5. **Loyalty partners** (concept-only until secured): *"Make every digital wait an earning moment."*

## 3. Voice
- Dry, warm, fast, Kiwi. Confident, never hypey. Short sentences; vary length.
- The mascot ("Dash", a long, patient, slightly cheeky dachshund) earns the playful lines: *"Long dog, short wait." · "Sit. Stay. Get paid." · "Wait. Wag. Earn."*
- **Banned words:** revolutionise, unlock, seamless, game-changer, supercharge, transform (as hype), "synergy."

## 4. Locked vocabulary
**Always:**
- "rewards, not cash" (cash is a later, gated tier — never lead with it)
- "opt-in" / "consent-first"
- "Privacy Act 2020 native · no prompts, content or files read"
- "two lines of SDK"
- "publishers keep 55%" (60% for the first three)
- "the wait" / "wait state" / "the agent is working"
- reward rails: **Airpoints · KiwiSaver · charity** (Everyday Rewards/power-bill/bank perk = "next"; cash = "coming soon")
- NZ-built · made by Assembl · Assembl-governed

**Avoid:**
- "ad network" as the lead noun (OK once, deep in body, as a long-term vision)
- "cash" / "cash back" as the headline promise
- promising any named loyalty partner (e.g. Air NZ) unless secured — frame as concept
- "banner", "pop-up", "video ad" (Dash is one quiet sponsored line)

## 5. Headlines to keep
- **Hero H1:** *Get paid to wait.* (icon line; "wait." carries the yellow highlight)
- Eyebrow: *Long dog. Short wait. NZ-built.*
- For AI builders: *Reward the time your **agent** spends working.*
- Publishers: *Your loading screen already runs millions of times. Make it pay.*
- Advertisers: *Buy New Zealand's most-viewed five seconds.*
- Rewards: *You choose where your wait goes.*
- Closing CTA: *The wait is already happening.*

## 6. Tagline bank (curated — use sparingly)
Overall: *Get a little back while you wait.* · *Turn waiting into earning.* · *From loading… to rewarding.*
Publishers: *Turn dead time into yield.* · *Your users are waiting. Your inventory is hiding.*
Advertisers: *Own the moment between click and result.* · *The cleanest five seconds in digital.*
People/mascot: *Wait. Wag. Earn.* · *No pop-ups. No videos. Just a better wait.*

## 7. Proof / trust strip (use real, specific claims)
- Opt-in · Privacy Act 2020 native · no prompts/content/files read
- One sponsor per slot, always labelled "Sponsored" · advertiser allow-list
- Hosted in AWS Sydney · made by Assembl (trust/oversight)
- Publishers keep 55% · two lines of SDK · fail-open (never a blank)

## 8. CTAs (consistent labels)
- Primary: **Join the waitlist** (charcoal pill, white text)
- AI builders: **Add it to your agent** / **For AI builders**
- Publishers: **Talk to us** / **Book a 20-min demo**
- Secondary/ghost: **See how it works** / **See the rewards**

## 9. Naming & wordmark
- Product: **Dash by Assembl**; the SDK pitch is the **"Dashhound Loader SDK"**.
- Wordmark: lowercase **dash–** (the dash is the brand mark; never "Dash." with a full stop in the logo).
- Mascot: **Dash** the dachshund (white + canary glossy render in the hero; charcoal+yellow line mark in the functional loader).

## 10. Visual alignment (so copy fits the system)
- Palette: **white** canvas · **charcoal** text (#353027) · **yellow** accents (#FFC400 / hi-vis #FFE600). No black, no green, no off-brand colours.
- Type: **Lato** everywhere (titles 700). Accent words = charcoal with a **yellow highlight underline**, never yellow text on white.
- Lots of whitespace; one idea per section; premium and calm.

---

*Keep copy specific over vague, problem before product, and let the dog be charming — not gimmicky. When in doubt: it's a loader that pays attention back.*
