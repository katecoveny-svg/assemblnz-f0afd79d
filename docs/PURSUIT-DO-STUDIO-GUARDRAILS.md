# Pursuit / DO / Studio guardrails

**Decision:** 17 September 2026  
**Status:** current product-architecture guardrail

The public Assembl structure is:

- **Pursuit — find it.**
- **DO — do it.**
- **Studio — show it.**

This simplification must not remove the deeper working products behind those three names.

## Pursuit client hubs are core

Pursuit client hubs must be preserved.

They are the private, client-specific working spaces where Assembl can hold and develop:

- company and buyer context;
- verified public signals and source trails;
- opportunities, tenders, awards and deadlines;
- relationship intelligence;
- opportunity briefs and next actions;
- client-specific journeys and concepts;
- interactive demonstrators and pitch material;
- drafts, review notes and approved outputs;
- reusable knowledge from previous work with that client.

The public `/pursuit` page explains the product. The Pursuit hub is the deeper working environment. Do not collapse the hub into a generic CRM, remove it in favour of a marketing landing page, or expose private client work publicly.

A client hub may be visually tailored to the client, while Assembl provenance, permission and truth labels remain clear.

## Studio is a full product, not a section

Studio must be preserved as a first-class product and working environment.

Studio is the visual, experiential and commercial production layer for:

- client-specific interactive demonstrators;
- websites and microsites;
- pitch experiences;
- customer-journey prototypes;
- image generation and art direction;
- video, film and motion;
- 3D and spatial experiences;
- advertising and campaign creative;
- social and sales assets;
- tender and proposal presentation;
- brand systems and creative direction.

Do not reduce Studio to a static “show” page, text file browser, gallery, or simple export function. It should remain genuinely visual, generative and capable of producing finished work.

## How the three connect

A strong client flow can be:

`signal → Pursuit client hub → DO → Studio → review / approval → proof → learning back into Pursuit`

But each product can also be sold and used independently.

- Pursuit can find and develop an opportunity without DO.
- DO can perform useful work without a Pursuit engagement.
- Studio can take an independent brief and create finished visual / commercial work without Pursuit or DO.

## What may be simplified

Public navigation, messaging and SEO may simplify the story to Pursuit / DO / Studio.

The following should be de-emphasised as competing top-level brands, not deleted when they remain useful:

- legacy pack / kete names;
- experimental agent names;
- wait-state and sponsored-journey terminology;
- Business Genome as a company-level proposition;
- internal Factory terminology;
- preview tools and labs.

These can remain capabilities, primitives, routes, experiments or internal infrastructure under the three-product architecture.

## Rule for future coding agents

Before deleting, redirecting, renaming or replacing any Pursuit client-hub or Studio workspace route, verify its current use and obtain an explicit product decision. A cleaner homepage is not permission to remove the working product behind it.
