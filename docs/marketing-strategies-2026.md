# assembl Marketing & Content Strategy (2026)

Prepared using Corey Haines' Marketing Skills Framework, aligned with the assembl V2 Brand Canon.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Positioning

**assembl** is an agentic customer journey platform that helps businesses automate complex customer experiences while maintaining human control, complete observability, and measured proof of success.

*   **Core Promise:** *Find the friction. Assemble the journey. Prove the result.*
*   **The Narrative:** "AI you can understand and see." Move away from "black box AI" or bare "AI chatbot" clichés. We build specialized team members (agents) working together to complete real journeys (e.g., flight rerouting, utility bill credits, GP prior approvals).
*   **Visual Persona:** Premium New York art gallery style. Deep navy base (`#080D1A`), gold/brass accents (`#B8964F`), liquid chrome 3D sculptural elements, massive asymmetrical layouts, and editorial serif headlines. (Demos are custom-branded for target companies, never dark-themed by default).

## 2. Multi-Platform Social Content Strategy

### A. LinkedIn: B2B Authority & Operational Excellence
*   **Primary Objective:** Position assembl as the enterprise gold-standard for agentic operations. Build trust among Founders, Chief Operating Officers (COOs), Customer Experience (CX) Leads, and Executive Assistants (EAs).
*   **Format:** In-depth text posts, multi-slide PDF carousels (NYC gallery aesthetic), and clean screen-captures of actual client journeys (observe/draft/approve states).
*   **Content Pillars:**
    1.  *Journey Economics (30%):* Explaining how companies lose customers in "the wait state" (e.g., waiting 4 days for an email reply) and how assembl bridges that wait state productively (e.g., earning bill credits while waiting).
    2.  *Agentic Frameworks (35%):* Educational breakdowns of the **Six Layers of assembl** (Business Genome → Customer Journey → Specialist Agent Team → Runtime → Wait State → Proof) and the **Authority Levels** (Observe, Draft, Recommend, Act with Approval, Autonomous with Audit).
    3.  *Behind-The-Build (20%):* Highlighting client wins (Everyday Rewards, AirNZ Standby, Contact Energy bill credits) and showing the visual "visual OS" in action.
    4.  *The 9am Brief (15%):* Operational tips for founders, showcasing how a messy stream of emails, calendar events, and notifications becomes a clean morning operational brief.
*   **Hook Formula Examples:**
    *   *Value Hook:* "Stop giving AI autonomous access to your customer database. Do this instead: implement the Observe → Draft → Approve contract."
    *   *Story Hook:* "Last week, a New Zealand utility customer hit a wait state. Instead of leaving in frustration, they accrued $4.20 toward their next power bill while their credit was calculated."

### B. Instagram: Premium Visual Gallery
*   **Primary Objective:** Build visual brand equity, show off our sculptural 3D identity, and present assembl as "Apple × Lego" for business operations.
*   **Format:** High-fidelity 1080x1080 graphic carousels using the **White Gallery / Paper-White Premium** theme (Paper `#FAFAF7`, dark navy `#1C1B18`, brass `#C9A45C` accents) and Reels of smoothly rotating WebGL sculptures.
*   **Key Visual Assets to Feature:**
    *   The **Liquid Chrome Blob / TorusKnot** central sculpture (the agent identity).
    *   The **Orbiting Elements** representing capabilities (e.g., glass boxes for knowledge, brass capsules for actions).
    *   Highly polished, rounded-corner mockups (28px card/phone radii) showing the live agent workspace.
*   **Creative Focus:** No neon grids or standard stock photos of brains. We treat software as sculpture. "Software disappears. Confidence remains."

## 3. TikTok Viral Strategy: "The 9am Brief" & "School Notices" Parser

TikTok is our primary engine for **demand creation** through free, high-utility tools (Engineering as Marketing). We will focus on two viral hooks: **"The 9am Brief"** (for founders, EAs, and solo-operators) and the **"School Notices" Parser** (for busy kiwi parents).

### A. The "School Notices" Parser (B2C Viral Hook)
*   **The Friction:** Every Kiwi parent experiences "Notice Board Whiplash." School notices come via separate emails, WhatsApp groups, newsletters, paper slips, and school apps. It's a logistical nightmare keeping track of sports practice, camp permissions, baking days, and uniform rules.
*   **The Free Tool:** Parents forward any messy email, photo of a paper slip, or copy-pasted app notice to a custom WhatsApp contact or upload it to a single-page web app. assembl parses it instantly, extracts the action items, and puts them in a clean **Weekly Whānau Calendar** with SMS reminders.
*   **TikTok Viral Format:**
    1.  *The Hook (0-3s):* Split screen. Top: A parent looking stressed, holding 4 paper slips and scrolling through 12 unread school emails. Bottom: "NZ parents, stop doing this. Forward it to this WhatsApp number instead."
    2.  *The Value (3-15s):* Record a phone screen using the WhatsApp interface. Forward a messy, 400-word newsletter containing details about cross-country running shoes and permission slips.
    3.  *The Result (15-25s):* Within 5 seconds, assembl sends back: "Here's what Jack needs: 1) Tuesday: Running shoes. 2) Thursday: $5 gold coin. 3) Friday: Consent form (link to sign)."
    4.  *Call to Action (25-30s):* "Free to use for Kiwi families. Go to `assembl.co.nz/toro` and try it."
*   **Why it goes viral:** It solves an emotionally charged, high-frequency personal pain point for an massive demographic (parents) who are highly active on social media and love sharing lifesaver parenting hacks.

### B. "The 9am Brief" (B2B Viral Hook)
*   **The Friction:** Small business owners, startup founders, and high-growth operators wake up to 80+ Slack messages, 40 unread emails, calendar conflicts, and task anxiety.
*   **The Free Tool:** assembl reads incoming streams, filters the noise, and delivers a beautifully structured morning brief at exactly 9am. It lists: "What occurred while you slept," "3 decisions requiring your approval today," and "Your scheduled focus blocks."
*   **TikTok Viral Format:**
    1.  *The Hook:* "I stopped checking Slack and email when I wake up. It saved me 15 hours a week and removed my morning anxiety."
    2.  *The Action:* Show the exact transition from a chaotic inbox of 100+ items to receiving "The 9am Brief by assembl" in a gorgeous, clean, glassmorphic layout.
    3.  *The Close:* "AI shouldn't send emails for you. It should prepare your drafts and organize your mind before you start the day."

## 4. Engineering-as-Marketing (Free Tool) Lead Nurture Loop

1.  **Low Barrier to Entry:** Fully ungated or "Email-only" partially gated. The parent/founder uploads a newsletter or drops in their email to get "The 9am Brief."
2.  **Immediate Delight:** Deliver high-fidelity, beautifully formatted outputs (draft briefs, formatted calendars) in under 8 seconds.
3.  **The Path to Paid (The "Make Yours" Path):**
    *   At the bottom of every 9am Brief: *"This brief was assembled by specialist agents. Want to connect these specialists to your actual CRM, Xero, or website? Book a 15-minute Pilot Sprint."*
    *   At the bottom of every School Notice Parser: *"This whānau automation runs on Tōro by assembl. Want custom automations for your business operations? Upgrade to an operator workspace."*
