## 1. LinkedIn Campaign (B2B Thought Leadership)

*   **Objective:** Target COOs, CX Directors, and Founders on the cost of customer waiting.
*   **Visual Asset:** The premium dark-gallery liquid-chrome gold-orbit sculpture (`assembl-intuitive-sculpture.png`).
*   **Format:** Text post + Image.

### Copy Template:
```markdown
Most businesses lose up to 32% of online leads at the exact same moment:

The wait state.

When a customer submits a home loan application, completes an insurance claim, or requests a complex billing reconciliation, your systems go to work. Under the hood, APIs are queried, data is parsed, and credit checks run. 

But to the customer? It’s a blank screen. An anxious loading spinner. 

At assembl, we believe the wait state shouldn't be friction. It should be a canvas. 

With "assembl intuitive agentic customer journeys", we transform idle waiting into a rewarded brand experience:

1.  Sovereign Transparency: The customer sees exactly which specialist agents are executing behind the scenes (e.g. "Verifying base salary... Drafting agreement...").
2.  Dynamic Credit Accrual: While they wait, the customer securely declares preferences and allergies, and accrues actual value live on their screen (e.g. $100 off establishment fees or 14 interest-free days).
3.  Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards: The AI specialists compile the draft agreements, but wait for a human operator’s thumbs-up signature before dispatching.

The result? 94% retention on applications, absolute SOC2 auditability, and immediate brand advocacy.

Because when you build with assembl intuitive, software disappears. Confidence remains.

Explore our personal finance and retail wait-state demonstrators live: www.assembl.co.nz

#assembl #AISEO #FintechNZ #CustomerExperience #EnterpriseOperations
```
