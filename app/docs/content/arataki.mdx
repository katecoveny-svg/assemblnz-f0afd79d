# Arataki

Arataki supports vehicle businesses where customer promises, finance disclosure, workshop status, and fleet records need a clean trail.

## Common workflows

- WoF or CoF status briefing.
- Customer enquiry response.
- Workshop job card draft.
- Vehicle listing compliance check.
- Finance disclosure draft.

## Core checks

- Privacy Act 2020.
- Consumer Guarantees Act 1993.
- Fair Trading Act 1986.
- Motor Vehicle Sales Act 2003.
- NZTA guidance.
