# Tōro

Tōro is the consumer product. It helps a family turn school notices, home admin, money reminders, and calendar pressure into reviewed drafts and weekly plans.

## Common workflows

- School notice triage.
- Weekly family plan.
- Grocery and meal draft.
- Budget reminder.
- Appointment and activity summary.

## Core checks

- Privacy Act 2020.
- Family safety posture.
- Consent and child-data handling.
- Operator review before sending.
