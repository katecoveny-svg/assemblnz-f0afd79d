# Auaha

Auaha helps creative and marketing teams move quickly while preserving claim evidence, usage rights, and campaign review.

## Common workflows

- Campaign claim check.
- Brand response draft.
- Social content calendar.
- Asset rights review.
- Performance summary.

## Core checks

- Fair Trading Act 1986.
- Copyright Act 1994.
- ASA codes.
- Privacy Act 2020.
- Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007.
