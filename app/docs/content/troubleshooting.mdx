# Troubleshooting

## No drafts in the inbox

- Confirm the tenant is active.
- Check that the kete fleet is enabled.
- Confirm the morning briefing runner has completed.
- Check filters in the inbox.

## Drafts sound wrong

Train the operator voice profile with five to ten sent emails, then mark drafts as needing your voice when they need rewriting.

## Xero or Google is disconnected

Reconnect the provider from onboarding or settings. The next briefing will use the refreshed context.

## A customer reply should not send

Reject the draft and include a reason. The rejection becomes part of the audit trail.
