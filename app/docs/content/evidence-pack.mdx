# Evidence packs

An evidence pack is the record behind a consequential output. It lets an operator, manager, auditor, or customer see the source material, checks, citations, and approval trail.

## What is included

- The request and operating context.
- The sources and legislation used.
- The Kahu, Iho, Tā, Mahara, and Mana trail.
- Operator approval history.
- Final output and citations.

## When to use it

Use an evidence pack when the output affects compliance, money, safety, privacy, customers, students, children, vehicles, shipments, or board records.

## Review posture

Agents draft. Operators approve. The pack should make the review faster without hiding the decision path.
