# Waihanga

Waihanga supports construction teams where proof matters: consent pathways, building-code checks, site safety, procurement, quality, and handover packs.

## Common workflows

- Consent application draft.
- Site safety plan.
- Variation or payment claim support.
- BIM and plan review summary.
- Quality closeout evidence pack.

## Core checks

- Building Act 2004.
- NZ Building Code.
- HSWA 2015.
- Construction Contracts Act 2002.
- WorkSafe guidance.
