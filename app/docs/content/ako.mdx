# Ako

Ako supports ECE operators with licensing, whānau communication, funding evidence, kaiako records, and ERO-ready documentation.

## Common workflows

- Licence condition check.
- RS7 and funding evidence summary.
- Incident record review.
- Whānau email draft.
- ERO evidence bundle.

## Core checks

- Education and Training Act 2020.
- Education ECE Services Regulations 2008.
- Licensing Criteria 2008.
- Children's Act 2014.
- Privacy Act 2020.
