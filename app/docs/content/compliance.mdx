# Compliance pipeline

Every consequential output moves through five named stages. The names are visible so your team can talk about the work clearly.

## Kahu

Kahu checks the request, masks sensitive detail where needed, and decides whether the work is safe to attempt.

## Iho

Iho routes the work to the right specialist and sets the context window.

## Tā

Tā drafts the work product and records the sources, rules, and assumptions used.

## Mahara

Mahara retrieves relevant business memory and avoids repeating known mistakes.

## Mana

Mana checks the output quality, cites the evidence, and prepares the operator review trail.
