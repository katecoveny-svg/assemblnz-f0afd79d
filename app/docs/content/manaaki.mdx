# Manaaki

Manaaki helps hospitality operators keep the guest experience moving while compliance records stay current.

## Common workflows

- Food control plan daily check.
- Temperature log review.
- Liquor licence renewal pack.
- Guest response draft.
- Roster and shift evidence summary.

## Core checks

- Food Act 2014.
- Sale and Supply of Alcohol Act 2012.
- HSWA 2015.
- Privacy Act 2020.
- MPI guidance.
