# Hoko

Hoko supports retailers with customer remedies, product claims, promotion checks, stock evidence, and supplier follow-up.

## Common workflows

- CGA response draft.
- Promotion claim check.
- Product safety note.
- Stock reorder briefing.
- Supplier issue summary.

## Core checks

- Consumer Guarantees Act 1993.
- Fair Trading Act 1986.
- Privacy Act 2020.
- Product safety standards.
- Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007.
