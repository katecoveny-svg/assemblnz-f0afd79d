# FAQ

## Does assembl send drafts automatically?

No. The operator reviews the draft first, then approves, edits, rejects, or defers it.

## Can one tenant run more than one kete?

Yes. Start with one primary kete, then add others when the team has a clear operating loop for them.

## Is the Industry Pack price per seat?

No. Industry Pack pricing is NZ$5,000/mo flat for the tenant.

## What is a Founding Pilot Sprint?

A ten-working-day, NZ$1,500 + GST once-off engagement for one workflow and one evidence pack, available to founding pilots.

## Can we cancel?

Yes. Industry Pack subscriptions can be cancelled.
