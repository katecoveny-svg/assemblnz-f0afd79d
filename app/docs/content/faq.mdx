# FAQ

## Does assembl send drafts automatically?

No. The operator reviews the draft first, then approves, edits, rejects, or defers it.

## Can one business run more than one agent?

Yes. Most people start with one install, then add agents when a different job needs covering. Team covers a few agents on one journey; Outcome is scoped when the work is bigger.

## Is pricing per seat?

No. Public pricing is install once, then a monthly keep-it-running or team fee — not seats. Outcome is priced on the work delivered. Current figures are on `/pricing`.

## What is a Founding Pilot Sprint?

A ten-working-day, NZ$1,500 + GST once-off engagement for one workflow and one evidence pack. It sits at the same entry price as the public install on `/pricing`.

## Can we cancel?

Yes. Keep-it-running and team are month to month; you keep the written record either way.
