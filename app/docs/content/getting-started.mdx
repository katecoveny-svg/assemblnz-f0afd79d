# Getting started

assembl installs agents that draft recurring work for New Zealand businesses — with a named person approving anything that reaches a customer. Start with one real job, keep the written record current, then add agents when another job starts annoying you.

## The first day

- Paste your website into the free builder, or pick an agent to try.
- Agree the install: written business record, one agent, one journey.
- Name the operator who will review drafts.
- Connect the tools that already hold your operating record when you need them.
- Review the first drafts and approve, edit, reject, or defer each one.

## What your operator sees

Every morning, drafts are grouped by agent and phase. The operator can batch approve routine items and flag anything that needs their voice.
