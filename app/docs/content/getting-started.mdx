# Getting started

assembl gives your team a specialist fleet for NZ work that needs proof. Start with one kete, connect the tools that already hold your operating record, then review drafts before anything leaves the business.

## The first day

- Pick the kete that matches the work you need to run.
- Create the tenant and choose the operator who will review drafts.
- Connect Stripe, Xero, Google Workspace, and any industry sources you use.
- Review the first inbox batch and approve, edit, reject, or defer each draft.

## What your operator sees

Every morning, drafts are grouped by agent and phase: Hunt, Pitch, Execution, and Ledger. The operator can batch approve routine items and flag anything that needs their voice.
