# Parent / Whānau Communication — quick reference skill (Ako)

[Kaihanga: scaffold. Draft full body before pilot.]

## When to use

[Kaihanga: write the trigger conditions — drafting a learning story
for a child, the term newsletter, a behavioural conversation memo
for a parent meeting, a sensitive incident-or-illness letter, an
exit letter, a fees / structural-change notice.]

## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT make a binding statement about a child's developmental
  stage, medical condition, behaviour diagnosis, or learning need.
  Those judgements sit with the kaiako team, the centre manager,
  and external specialists.
- Will NOT post directly to Storypark / Educa / Hero / the centre's
  parent portal on the centre's behalf. Publishing remains with
  the kaiako or the centre administrator.
- Will NOT exclude a child from the centre, issue an exit letter,
  or close out a complaint on the centre's behalf. Decisions
  remain with the licensed person responsible.
- Will NOT respond on the centre's behalf to an ERO concern, a
  Ministry of Education enquiry, an Oranga Tamariki query, or a
  parental Privacy Act request. Drafts go to the centre manager
  and the centre's professional adviser.
- Will NOT use the bare token "AI" in customer-facing copy
  (canon hard rule #4). Uses "intelligent automation" or describes
  the function.

## Tikanga check

[Kaihanga: write the tikanga frame — whānau communication is
relational. For Māori, Pasifika, and migrant whānau, defer to
tikanga-compliance for cultural protocols (kanohi-ki-te-kanohi
preference for difficult conversations, whakapapa-aware language,
reo Māori greetings and closures). Reserved taonga terms not used
in newsletter copy. Macrons enforced in tamariki names.]

## Privacy Act check

[Kaihanga: write the IPP coverage — newsletters, learning stories,
and parent letters contain personal information of tamariki
(highly sensitive — minors, photos). IPP 1 (collect only what the
purpose requires), IPP 5 (parent portal access controls), IPP 8
(accuracy — every fact about a child verified before publication),
IPP 9 (retention per Education and Training Act / records-management),
IPP 11 (disclosure beyond the parent — strictly controlled),
IPP 3A (1 May 2026) for indirectly collected information.]

## Workflow steps

[Kaihanga: write the step-by-step workflow — receive the
communication input (event / topic / audience), draft against the
