# ECE Curriculum Plan — quick reference skill (Ako)

[Kaihanga: scaffold. Draft full body before pilot.]

## When to use

[Kaihanga: write the trigger conditions — drafting the centre's
long-term plan against Te Whāriki, drafting a term plan or a
week-by-week plan, drafting an individual learning story / ILP,
preparing curriculum evidence for an ERO review.]

## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT make a binding determination on a child's developmental
  stage, learning need, or referral to specialist services. Those
  judgements sit with the kaiako team, the centre manager, and
  external specialists (RTLB, Ministry of Education learning
  support).
- Will NOT submit any document to the Education Review Office
  (ERO) or the Ministry of Education on the centre's behalf.
  Submissions remain with the licensed person responsible.
- Will NOT advise on whether a centre meets the licensing criteria
  under the Education (Early Childhood Services) Regulations 2008.
  Refer to the centre's professional adviser or licensing officer.
- Will NOT respond on the centre's behalf to a parent / whānau
  concern, an ERO review finding, or a Ministry of Education
  enquiry. Drafts go to the centre manager.
- Will NOT use the bare token "AI" in customer-facing curriculum
  copy (canon hard rule #4). Uses "intelligent automation" or
  describes the function.

## Tikanga check

[Kaihanga: write the tikanga frame — Te Whāriki is itself bicultural
and tikanga-grounded. Curriculum plans for kōhanga reo and
bilingual ECE centres defer to tikanga-compliance for all te reo
content; whakapapa and tikanga of the local hapū / iwi inform any
mātauranga Māori reference. Reserved taonga terms are not used in
learning-story copy without consent. Macrons enforced.]

## Privacy Act check

[Kaihanga: write the IPP coverage — learning stories and ILPs
contain personal information of tamariki (highly sensitive — minors,
photos, learning needs, health information). IPP 1, IPP 5 (parent /
whānau access controls; centre portal and shared-drive permissions),
IPP 9 (retention per Education and Training Act and Children's Act),
IPP 11 (disclosure to specialists — only with parent consent),
IPP 3A (1 May 2026) where information is collected indirectly from
a previous centre / kindergarten / specialist.]

## Workflow steps

[Kaihanga: write the step-by-step workflow — receive the planning
input from the kaiako team, draft the plan against the centre's
Te Whāriki interpretation, integrate child voice and whānau
aspirations from the latest learning conversations, run the
tikanga and privacy passes, stage for the centre manager.]
