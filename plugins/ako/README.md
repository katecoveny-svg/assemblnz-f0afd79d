---

Ako is the assembl plugin family for early childhood education operators in Aotearoa — ECE centres, kōhanga reo, home-based educators, and Pasifika early-learning services. The kete drafts curriculum plans against Te Whāriki, child-safety reviews under the Children's Act 2014 and Education (Early Childhood Services) Regulations 2008, and parent / whānau communications. Drafts only — every output is staged for the centre manager, the licensed person responsible, the safety check officer, or the ECE-network professional adviser to review and sign before any document goes to ERO, the Ministry of Education, or a parent.

## Skills (this PR — scaffold)

- `ece-curriculum-plan` — Te Whāriki-aligned long-term, term, and weekly plans.
- `child-safety-review` — Children's Act safety check drafts, incident-report drafts, hazard-and-risk register entries.
- `parent-communication` — daily learning stories, term newsletters, parent-meeting notes, sensitive communication drafts.
