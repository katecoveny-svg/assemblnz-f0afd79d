crew plan, drafting the on-set H&S plan for sign-off by the H&S-
competent person.]

## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT lodge a council filming permit, a DOC concession, or
  any iwi-consent application on the operator's behalf. Lodgement
  remains with the location manager.
- Will NOT make a binding on-set safety determination under HSWA
  2015 — call sheets and risk assessments are draft work for the
  on-set H&S-competent person to review and sign.
- Will NOT bind a location, a talent contract, a music sync, or a
  post house on the operator's behalf. Binding remains with the
  producer.
- Will NOT respond on the operator's behalf to an iwi-consent
  query, a council compliance officer's notice, or a DOC concession
  audit. Drafts go to the producer and the operator's solicitor.
- Will NOT submit ScreenSafe / Film Safety Council reports on the
  operator's behalf. Submissions remain with the production
  manager.

## Tikanga check

[Kaihanga: write the tikanga frame — shoots on whenua Māori,

mātauranga Māori, and shoots involving Māori talent / kaumātua
require tikanga-compliance review. Iwi consent is sought
relationally, not as a paperwork checkbox.]

## Privacy Act check

[Kaihanga: write the IPP coverage — call sheets contain personal
information of crew and talent (phone, address, dietary
requirements, emergency contact). IPP 1, IPP 5 (call-sheet
distribution access controls), IPP 9 (retention per ScreenSafe /
production-archive practice), IPP 11 (disclosure to insurer, to
location owner, to the post house), IPP 3A (1 May 2026) for
indirectly collected location-owner / supplier information.]
