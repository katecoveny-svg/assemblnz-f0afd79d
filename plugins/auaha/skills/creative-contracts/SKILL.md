# Creative Contracts — quick reference skill (Auaha)

[Kaihanga: scaffold. Draft full body before pilot.]

## When to use

[Kaihanga: write the trigger conditions — drafting a new SoW for
a campaign, drafting an IP-assignment for a brand identity job,
drafting a talent / model release for a shoot, drafting a
contractor agreement for a freelance creative.]

## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT make a binding determination on whether a copyright
  work qualifies for "first ownership" by the commissioning party
  under Copyright Act s.21 or s.94, or whether moral rights
  (ss.94–98) have been validly waived. Refer to the operator's
  IP solicitor.
- Will NOT execute contracts, send DocuSign envelopes, or transmit
  signed PDFs on the operator's behalf. Execution remains with
  the producer / contracts manager and the named human signatory.
- Will NOT advise on tax or contractor-vs-employee status under the
  Employment Relations Act 2000 or the Income Tax Act 2007. Refer
  to a tax accountant or employment-law solicitor.
- Will NOT respond on the operator's behalf to an Employment
  Relations Authority claim, an IP infringement allegation, or a
  contract-breach demand. Drafts go to the operator's solicitor.
- Will NOT release a counterparty, a talent agent, or a music
  publisher from a written agreement. Variations and releases stay
  with the named signatory.

## Tikanga check

[Kaihanga: write the tikanga frame — contracts engaging Māori
artists, kapa haka groups, taonga performers, or work touching
te ao Māori cultural property require tikanga-compliance review.
Whakapapa, kaitiaki obligations, and tangata whenua consent
norms are honoured in the licensing terms; reserved taonga terms
are not assigned in standard buyout language.]

## Privacy Act check

[Kaihanga: write the IPP coverage — talent releases collect
performer personal information (full name, IRD number for tax
withholding, emergency contact). IPP 1, IPP 5, IPP 9, IPP 11
(disclosure to PA / agency / payroll), IPP 3A (1 May 2026) where
performer information is collected indirectly from a casting
agent.]
