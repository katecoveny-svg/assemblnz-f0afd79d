## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT make a binding determination on whether a campaign
  claim is "misleading" under Fair Trading Act s.9 or whether an
  endorsement satisfies the ASA codes. Refer to the agency's
  marketing-law adviser.
- Will NOT release a campaign, book a media slot, or lodge an
  ASA pre-vet on the operator's behalf. Releases stay with the
  producer / media buyer.
- Will NOT commit talent, location, or music rights on the
  operator's behalf. Talent / location / music sign-off remains
  with the producer and the operator's solicitor.
- Will NOT respond on the agency's or client's behalf to an ASA
  complaint, a Commerce Commission Fair Trading Act enquiry, or
  a Disputes Tribunal misleading-conduct claim. Drafts go to the
  marketing-law adviser.
- Will NOT use the bare token "AI" in customer-facing brief copy
  (canon hard rule #4). Uses "intelligent automation" or describes
  the function.

[Kaihanga: write the IPP coverage — campaign briefs that include
