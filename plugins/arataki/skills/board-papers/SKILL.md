# Board Papers — quick reference skill (Arataki)

## When to use

[Kaihanga: write the trigger conditions — drafting the agenda for
the next board meeting, drafting a decision paper for a proposed
director appointment / capital raise / strategic pivot, drafting
resolutions and minutes shells, drafting AGM materials.]

## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT make a binding determination on a director's duty under
  Companies Act ss.131–137 (good faith, proper purpose, care and
  skill, disclosure of interests, reckless trading). That sits with
  the chair, the company secretary, and the company's solicitor.
- Will NOT file an annual return, change-of-director notice, or
  shareholder resolution with the Companies Office. Filings remain
  with the company secretary.
- Will NOT respond on the company's behalf to an FMA, NZX, or
  Charities Services enquiry. Drafts go to the chair and the
  company's solicitor for review.
- Will NOT certify a quorum, close out a board resolution, or sign
  the minutes. Sign-off remains with the chair and the company
  secretary.
- Will NOT determine whether a director has a material conflict
  under s.139 or whether the disclosure has been adequate. Refer
  to the company's solicitor.

## Tikanga check

[Kaihanga: write the tikanga frame — Māori-trust boards, iwi
governance entities, and post-settlement governance entities have
specific tikanga around whakapapa-based representation, kaitiaki
appointments, and decision-by-rūnanga. The skill defers to
tikanga-compliance for any draft paper / minute / resolution
touching te ao Māori governance contexts. Macrons enforced.]

## Privacy Act check

[Kaihanga: write the IPP coverage — board papers contain personal
information of directors (interests register, addresses), of staff
(remuneration committee items), and sometimes of customers
(monitoring papers). IPP 1, IPP 5 (board portal access controls,
in-committee handling), IPP 9 (retention per Companies Act and
Trusts Act record-keeping), IPP 11 (disclosure to auditors,
regulators, the chair's solicitor), IPP 3A (1 May 2026) for
indirectly collected stakeholder data.]
