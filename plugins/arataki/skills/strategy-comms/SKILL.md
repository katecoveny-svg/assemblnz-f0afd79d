## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT release a continuous-disclosure announcement to NZX, an
  FMA disclosure, or a Charities Services notice on the entity's
  behalf. Release is the company secretary's or the IR adviser's.
- Will NOT make a determination on whether information is "material"
  under NZX Listing Rule 3 or the FMA Act. Refer to the company's
  IR adviser and / or solicitor.
- Will NOT respond on the entity's behalf to a journalist, a
  regulator query, or a shareholder Section 178 request. Drafts
  go to the chair and the IR / comms adviser.
- Will NOT post to the entity's social-media accounts, send mass
  email through the entity's MAS / CRM, or publish through the
  company's investor-relations portal. Publishing remains with the
  comms operator.
- Will NOT use the bare token "AI" in customer-facing copy (canon
  hard rule #4). Uses "intelligent automation" or describes the
  function.
