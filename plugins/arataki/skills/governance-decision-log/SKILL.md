[Kaihanga: write the trigger conditions — adding a new directorship

for a board paper, creating a decision-log entry after a board
resolution, drafting a delegation-register update, drafting a
risk-register or compliance-matrix entry for the audit committee.]

## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT make a binding determination on whether a director's
  interest is "material" under Companies Act s.139 or whether the
  director must abstain from a vote under s.140. Those judgements
  sit with the chair, the company secretary, and the company's
  solicitor.
- Will NOT file or update the Companies Office director / shareholder
  / address register on the entity's behalf. Filings remain with the
  company secretary.
- Will NOT certify a delegation boundary, close out a risk-register
  item, or sign off on the compliance matrix. Sign-off remains with
  the chair, the audit committee chair, or the responsible executive.
- Will NOT respond on the entity's behalf to a Companies Office
  enquiry, an FMA review, or a Charities Services audit. Drafts
  go to the chair and the company's solicitor.
- Will NOT replace the entity's existing GRC system of record. The
  decision log drafted here is a working document staged for entry
  into the entity's primary register.

## Privacy Act check

[Kaihanga: write the IPP coverage — interests-register entries
contain personal information of directors and their related parties
(family members, family trusts). IPP 1 (collection limited to what
the Companies Act / Trusts Act requires), IPP 5 (board portal access
controls), IPP 9 (retention per Companies Act / Trusts Act), IPP 11
(disclosure to auditors, regulators, lenders), IPP 3A (1 May 2026)
for indirectly collected related-party information.]
