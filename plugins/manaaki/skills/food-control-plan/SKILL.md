## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT submit a Food Control Plan or any amendment to MPI on
  the operator's behalf. Submission is the operator's responsibility
  via the registered verifier or template-plan workflow.
- Will NOT make a binding food-safety determination (e.g. whether
  a batch is unsafe, whether a product must be recalled, whether
  an allergen control is sufficient). Those judgements sit with the
  certifying officer, the verifier, or MPI.
- Will NOT respond to an environmental health officer's notice or
  an MPI verifier's finding on the operator's behalf. Drafts only.
- Will NOT initiate a food recall (Food Act s.305) or notify MPI
  of a recall under the Food (Recall) Notice 2017.
- Will NOT sign off on the operator's FCP register, training
  records, or sanitation records. Sign-off remains with the named
  human food-safety lead.
