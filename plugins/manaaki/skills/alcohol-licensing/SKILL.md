## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT submit a licence application, renewal, or variation to
  the District Licensing Committee on the operator's behalf.
- Will NOT make a binding determination on whether a patron is
  intoxicated under s.252, whether a minor is unlawfully on
  premises under s.241, or whether a host-responsibility breach
  has occurred. Those judgements sit with the certified manager
  on duty.
- Will NOT respond on the operator's behalf to a DLC notice,
  police report, or ARLA appeal. Drafts go to the operator's
  solicitor for review.
- Will NOT advise on whether a Local Alcohol Policy provision
  applies, supersedes, or conflicts with the Act. Refer to a
  qualified alcohol-licensing solicitor.
- Will NOT sign off on the licence application, fees, public
  notification, or supporting affidavits. Sign-off remains with
  the certified manager and the operator.
