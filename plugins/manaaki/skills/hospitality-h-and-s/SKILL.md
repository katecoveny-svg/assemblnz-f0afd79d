## When to use

[Kaihanga: write the trigger conditions — drafting the kitchen
hazard register, drafting a safe operating procedure for a new
piece of gas or fryer equipment, drafting an incident record after
a slip / burn / cut, preparing a notifiable-event narrative for
the H&S-competent person to review.]

## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT submit a notifiable-event report to WorkSafe under
  HSWA 2015 ss.56–58. Notification is the PCBU's duty and goes
  through WorkSafe's online form by a competent person.
- Will NOT make a binding determination on whether an event is
  "notifiable" under s.23 (notifiable injury or illness) or s.24
  (notifiable incident). Triage drafts only — the H&S-competent
  person decides.
- Will NOT respond to a WorkSafe inspector's improvement, prohibition,
  or infringement notice on the operator's behalf. Drafts go to the
  PCBU's officer or solicitor.
- Will NOT certify a hazard control as adequate, sign off on a safe
  operating procedure, or close out a corrective action. Sign-off
  remains with the named H&S-competent person.
- Will NOT lodge an ACC claim, submit ACC injury reports, or
  communicate with ACC on the worker's behalf. ACC interactions
  are the worker's (and ACC's case manager's) responsibility.
