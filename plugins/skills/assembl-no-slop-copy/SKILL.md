# Assembl copy review

Read docs/assembl-copy-standard.md and current user instructions first. This skill adds a practical pass; it does not rename products or override verified runtime facts.

1. Identify the reader, job, output and next action. State each in concrete words.
2. Separate live, trial, disabled, draft and proposed. A connection or model response is not completed external work.
3. Remove filler: quiet, quietly, seamless, effortlessly, unlock, unleash, elevate, revolutionary, game-changing, cutting-edge, world-class, ecosystem, synergy. Do not remove such a word from a legal name or quoted source without checking context.
4. Replace abstract promises with the actual task: research a company, check a source, prepare a brief, export a pitch.
5. Remove unsupported numbers, endorsements, client relationships, budget and demand claims. Keep uncertainty alongside the claim.
6. Prefer a full stop or comma over habitual em dashes. Use sentence case, short paragraphs and varied sentence lengths. Do not substitute endless three-word slogans for explanation.
7. Keep Pursuit / DO / Studio and lowercase assembl. Fonts and colours are defined by brand canon, not this skill.
8. Check buttons against actual destinations. Public product pages must not silently enter private client hubs.
9. Read the result aloud. Remove repetition and technical implementation commentary from the customer interface.
10. Run targeted copy checks and inspect the rendered page, including unavailable/error states. Automated word checks are a backstop, not a copywriter.

Public research prompt must include this style and evidence policy. Do not rewrite or censor cited source material as though it were the agent's own copy.
