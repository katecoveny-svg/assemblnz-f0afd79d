# Hoko — retail and e-commerce industry pack

> Nothing in this repository constitutes legal, tax, accounting, financial, immigration, customs, biosecurity, or health and safety advice. These agents draft work product — entries, memos, checklists, calculations, draft correspondence — for review by a qualified professional or licensed adviser. They do not file with Inland Revenue, lodge customs entries, submit WorkSafe notifications, make ACC claims, register entities with the Companies Office, send Privacy Commissioner breach notifications, or submit any document to a NZ government agency on the user's behalf; every output is staged for human sign-off. The user is responsible for verifying outputs and for compliance with all applicable New Zealand laws, including but not limited to the Privacy Act 2020, the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, the Fair Trading Act 1986, the AML/CFT Act 2009, the Customs and Excise Act 2018, the Building Act 2004, the Construction Contracts Act 2002, the Health and Safety at Work Act 2015, and any tikanga Māori obligations relevant to their work.

---

Hoko is the assembl plugin family for retail and e-commerce operators in Aotearoa — physical store owners, online retailers, marketplaces, direct-to-consumer brands, and supplier-resellers. The kete drafts consumer-law checks, returns and refund policies, and marketing-claims reviews against the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, the Fair Trading Act 1986 (s.9 misleading-conduct test), and the Privacy Act 2020 as applied to retail data handling. Drafts only — every output is staged for the operator, the operator's solicitor, or the marketing-law adviser to review and sign before any policy is published or any customer letter is sent.

## Skills (this PR — scaffold)

- `consumer-law-check` — CGA / FTA quick reference, customer-complaint triage drafts, Disputes Tribunal preparation drafts.
- `returns-policy` — returns-and-refund policy drafts, distance-selling cooling-off drafts, gift-card and store-credit terms.
- `marketing-claims-review` — pricing-claim, comparison-claim, "free-from" / "natural" / "NZ-made" claim review drafts; ASA pre-vet drafts.
