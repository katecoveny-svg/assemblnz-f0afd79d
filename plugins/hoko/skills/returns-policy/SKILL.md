## What this skill will NOT do

- Will NOT make a binding determination on whether a returns clause
  contracts out of CGA rights (CGA s.43 — generally cannot, in B2C).
  Refer to the operator's solicitor.
- Will NOT publish the policy to the operator's website, point of
  sale, or platform listings (Shopify / Trade Me / Amazon NZ /
  Marketplace) on the operator's behalf. Publishing remains with
  the operator.
- Will NOT respond on the operator's behalf to a customer-rights
  challenge, a Disputes Tribunal hearing, a Commerce Commission
  Fair Trading Act enquiry, or a Consumer NZ enquiry. Drafts go
  to the operator and the operator's solicitor.
- Will NOT advise on tax or GST treatment of refunds, store-credit
  expiry, or gift-card redemption. Refer to the operator's
  accountant.
- Will NOT auto-issue a refund through Stripe / Shopify / Afterpay
  / payment gateway. Refunds are issued by the operator's named
  human.
