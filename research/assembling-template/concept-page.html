02 · the foundation · three parts, one exchange

small on purpose. three parts.

Each with one job. The customer sees the reward before they choose. The client keeps the record. Assembl earns only on completed value.

01 · the concept
the moment.
One optional, clearly-rewarded moment inside an approved digital wait. Featured wait: the weekly online shop planning window. Everything is opt-in.
02 · the approval
the boundary.
Assembling can prepare — never purchase, redeem or substitute. Every consequential action stays with Woolworths' operations team and the customer.
03 · the exchange
the split.
One completed wait = $0.40 illustrative. 55% to Woolworths · 30% to the member · 15% to assembl.
03 · the counterfactual

what happens across 2 million+ members if nothing is piloted.

Everyday Rewards NZ members · verified
2M+
Today, a member opens the app to plan the weekly shop, browses for anywhere between 5 and 40 minutes, and the app returns no assembled value. This is the baseline. The concept is what happens if 500 opted-in members, in a 4-week sandbox, get their week prepared for review instead. Small enough to run this quarter. Big enough to prove or fail cleanly.

06 · the pilot ask

the smallest credible next step.

01 · the scope
One weekly-online-shop journey · 500–1,000 opted-in Everyday Rewards members · a 4-week sandbox.
02 · the access
Read-only catalogue + household context. Draft-write only for the basket. No writes to the rewards ledger in phase 1 — staged credit for phase-2 review.
03 · the scorecard
≥30 min returned per member per week · ≥60% completion of opted-in wait moments · ≥30% basket-review rate · 0 unapproved actions.

fail any line of the scorecard and we change the design or stop.
