How the moment works

  1. The signalSomething you already record says a person is now waiting, and roughly how long for.

  4. The exchangeThey get value. You get someone who stayed, and one optional answer you have always wanted.

US retailers lose an estimated $37.7 billion a year to customers who abandon because of long queues, split roughly $15.8b to competitors and $21.9b abandoned outright (Adyen with 451 Research). The money, in plain words

Summerset publishes both halves of this.
Never together.

The deferred management fee is 25% over four years — published on the website. Expected tenure for a villa is seven to eight years — published in the Annual Report, to investors. Read either one alone and a family imagines a partial fee. Put them side by side and the honest answer appears: for the average villa resident, accrual finished years ago. It is the whole 25%. Nothing here is invented; it is two of your own numbers, in one place, for the first time.

    The two halves, on one axis

    the fee accruing fully accrued, and staying there expected tenure

    It cannot compound across moves

    “Your Deferred Management Fee will not exceed 25% overall (where the standard Deferred Management Fee applies)”, with the percentage already accrued carried across. This is the reassuring half, and it is buried.

    Statutory disclosure statement, Retirement Villages Register.
    A health-driven move costs nothing upfront

    “If your health needs necessitate a transfer into a Serviced Apartment or Care Suite we will not require you to make any additional upfront payment in order to make the transfer.” The 2% transfer administration fee is waived for exactly those moves.

    Summerset, published. Transfer fee 2% of the licence payment for the new home.
    If one of a couple moves and one stays

    The Dual Occupancy Fee is “currently capped at 10% of the Licence Payment for the Care Suite, with 5% accruing on the Commencement Date and a further 5% accruing on day 366”, and joint residents are never charged more than one fully accrued Dual Occupancy Fee.

    Statutory disclosure statement. This question is asked constantly and answered nowhere a family looks.
    The weekly fee is tied to NZ Super

    It increases annually on 1 July, and the increase “will never exceed the percentage increase in the after tax rate of New Zealand Superannuation in that year”. It stops when she vacates her home. This is the single most reassuring fact Summerset has, and it is in a 42-page PDF on a Companies Office register.

    Summerset on the Landing disclosure statement (Summerset Villages (Kenepuru) Ltd, 1 Jan 2026). Village-specific until confirmed estate-wide.

    Whose arithmetic this is. The 25%, the four-year accrual, the tenure ranges and every clause above are Summerset’s own published figures.     The boundary

    What this concept does not do.

    No production access is requested by this concept. No resident record, no care plan and no clinical data is involved or asked for. Everything here is built from what Summerset already publishes, plus what a family types into an enquiry form. Scope, privacy, security and commercial terms would need joint approval before any pilot began.

    One region’s information-pack enquiries. Four weeks. Opt-in only.

    02 · the access

    Read-only public content plus what the family types. Draft-write only to the pack. Nothing written to any Summerset system.

    03 · the scorecard

    Enquiry-to-visit rate up, time to a first useful answer down, zero unapproved sends.

    Fail any line of the scorecard and we change the design or stop.

    One village and one decision journey, for six weeks — from the information pack through to the ninety days after moving in. Not the whole portfolio, and nothing to do with care.

    Your published information pack, the fee documents and the Summerset Sure terms, and a named person in sales. No resident data, no care records, nothing that writes.
