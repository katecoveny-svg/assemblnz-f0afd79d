What we are actually asking

What this will never do

It does not send. It drafts, and a named person sends. It does not publish, write back to Braze, buy media, commit spend, or contact a member directly. It never uses Trade Me data — every figure and journey on this page is illustrative. And where the right answer is to say nothing, it says nothing: a win-back after a listing that did not sell is suppressed, not sent.

No production access is requested by this concept.

The pilot ask

Scope. One lifecycle stage — retention, the vendor conversation — for six weeks. Not acquisition campaigns, not the consumer side, not pricing.

Access. Listing performance fields and your comparable-set definition. No member records, no enquirer details, no Braze connection.

Scorecard. Journeys shipped without rewrite. Suppressions a person agreed with. One lifecycle lead's answer to whether they would keep it.

Fail any line of the scorecard and we change the design or stop.
