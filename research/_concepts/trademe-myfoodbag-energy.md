# Sector concepts — Trade Me Property · My Food Bag · Energy retail (researched 27 Jul 2026)

## Trade Me Property
- Wait that matters: the silence after "Apply" on a rental (days, often no reply; Jan demand +41%).
- Earn: "move-in credits" per verified waiting minute, redeemable on Trade Me Marketplace (movers buy the couch/fridge anyway).
- One question: "When do you actually need to move?" → triage + routing.
- WOW: **watch your key being cut** — a brass key gains a notch per completed check (identity, references, credit, viewing); it turns in the lock on approval.
- Facts: median advertised rent $620/wk Jan 2026 (interest.co.nz/property/137403); Jan tenant demand +41% vs Dec, listings +45% (trademe.co.nz/c/property/news/rental-price-index); digital applications via partner Renti (trademe.co.nz/c/property/article/listing-your-rental-property-on-trade-me-a-guide).

## My Food Bag
- Wait: delivery day — order locks Mon 11:59pm, box lands Sat–Mon; the delivery-afternoon window is where takeaways steal the night.
- Earn: "kitchen credit" per waiting minute, redeemable only on next-box add-ons (retention + basket growth).
- One question: "Which night are you most tempted by takeaways?" → competitor intel + menu targeting.
- WOW: **watch tonight's dinner assemble** — 3D bench, real ingredients land as agents confirm sourcing/subs; week's recipes reshuffle live around the takeaway-night answer.
- Facts: FY26 revenue $170.2m +5%, NPAT $6.7m (MFB annual report FY26 PDF); active customers 57,100 H2 FY26; no skips after Mon 11:59pm (myfoodbag.co.nz/how-it-works).

## Energy retail (Electric Kiwi)
- Wait: switch limbo — ~10–15 business days ICP transfer, zero visibility, win-back calls land mid-gap.
- Earn: waiting minutes → bonus Hour of Power minutes (native currency, zero new mechanics).
- One question: "EV, heat pump or solar — have or planning?" → day-zero plan routing.
- WOW: **choose your free hour against tonight's actual grid** — dial slides Hour of Power along the real demand curve; the house lights up; projected saving recalculates live.
- Facts: Powerswitch traffic +75% YoY Jul 2025, switches initiated +170% (1news.co.nz 2025/08/27); EA rules from 30 Oct 2026 — back-bills capped 6 months, annual best-plan checks (ea.govt.nz press release 3 Mar 2026); free daily self-chosen Hour of Power (electrickiwi.co.nz).
