# Sector concepts — NZ Post · Air NZ · Woolworths (researched 27 Jul 2026)

## NZ Post
- Wait: "out for delivery" day — tracking flips at breakfast then silence to ~6pm; missed delivery = 1–3 day loop.
- Earn: delivery credits per waited minute → postage/satchels or a priority redelivery window (NZ Post has NO consumer loyalty scheme — first-mover).
- Question: "Safe place to leave it next time?" → kills the most expensive failure mode.
- WOW: **live 3D parcel through the real network** — lodgement → Wiri sorter → depot → a van glyph down the customer's own street, real scan events lighting nodes.
- Facts: FY24/25 $2m net loss, $12m YoY improvement (nzpost.co.nz media); Wiri sorts 30,000+ parcels/hr (nzpost.co.nz); ~2.2m parcels/week at Christmas peak (nzpost.co.nz).

## Air NZ (generic airline demo context)
- Wait: cancellation → new itinerary gap; engine-constrained fleet makes cancellations structural; hour+ queues, zero visibility.
- Earn: Airpoints-Dollar-style micro-credits per disrupted minute + a status gesture (maps to Apr 2026 "Koru" relaunch).
- Question: "What matters most for the rest of this trip — earliest arrival, group together, fewest connections?"
- WOW: **three candidate itineraries assemble live on a koru route map** — seats held, legs drawing themselves; customer taps preference; human approves before ticketing.
- Facts: H1 FY26 $40m net loss, fleet constraints stated (airnewzealandnewsroom.com); up to 11 jets grounded, NZ$129m compensation FY25 (flightglobal.com); loyalty relaunched as "Koru" Apr 2026, 5m+ members (airnewzealandnewsroom.com).

## Woolworths NZ (grocery)
- Wait: the picking window — same-day pick happening RIGHT NOW (23% of online orders within 2 hours); swaps decided without the shopper.
- Earn: Everyday Rewards points per engaged minute toward the existing $15 voucher.
- Question: "Anything you wanted today you couldn't find?" → missed-basket demand data.
- WOW: **watch your trolley picked live** — aisle-by-aisle store map, items dropping into a 3D trolley per scan, each out-of-stock raises a swap card the customer taps.
- Facts: Q3 FY25 NZ eCom +24.3% to $313m, 23% ≤2hrs (woolworthsgroup.com.au PDF); Everyday Rewards NZ 2.1m active members (same); 1pt/$1, 2,000pts = $15 voucher (account.woolworths.co.nz).
