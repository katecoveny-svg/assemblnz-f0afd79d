# Sector concepts — Nectar/Instant Finance · Sharesies · Hnry · Banking (researched 27 Jul 2026)

## Nectar × Instant Finance (lending)
- Wait: 7-minute quote → money landing (statement read, affordability, e-sign, payout; same-day at best). Borrower under pressure, quietly ashamed.
- Earn: wait-minutes → dollars off the $240 establishment fee.
- Question: "Did you apply anywhere else for this loan this week?"
- WOW: **watch your own bank statement being read** — transactions sort into essentials/discretionary/debt lanes while a "reasonable inquiries" checklist lights green; human stamp last.
- Facts: 7-min quotes, $1k–$30k, 7.95–29.95%, $240 fee (nectar.co.nz); Instant Finance since 1971, 25 branches; Symon Nausbaum directs both (companyhub.nz); CCCFA prescriptive affordability rules scrapped 31 Jul 2024 (beehive.govt.nz).

## Sharesies (investing)
- Wait: KiwiSaver transfer — up to 10 working days where retirement savings go dark.
- Earn: each transfer day → a fee-free buy or plan-fee credit ("the wait funds your first trades").
- Question: "First home or retirement?" → suitability + lifetime value.
- WOW: **your money in transit** — old provider → IRD → cash lands, while the chosen portfolio assembles slice by slice the moment funds clear.
- Facts: 1m+ members, $12b on platform May 2026 (investmentnews.co.nz); transfers ≤10 business days (Sharesies help); KiwiSaver scheme $900m/~27k members (investmentnews.co.nz).

## Hnry (tax/fintech)
- Wait: EOFY — return filed, IRD assessing; days-to-weeks of "refund or bill?" in silence.
- Earn: fee-back credits — basis points off the 1% fee on the next invoice.
- Question: "Did any income this year NOT go through Hnry?" (the accuracy question they can't comfortably ask).
- WOW: **the year rewind** — 12 months of invoices land and split live into tax/GST/ACC/student-loan streams → one number: "Refund: $412. Already cleared."
- Facts: ~1% of IRD's tax income flows through Hnry (hnry.co.nz); 1% +GST capped $1,500 (MoneyHub); $30m+ Series C Dec 2025 (RNZ).

## NZ retail banking (generic)
- Wait: home-loan approval against an offer/tender deadline; Ombudsman found even 3 months "reasonable".
- Earn: wait-days accrue a cash credit paid at drawdown (visibly EARNED cash-back).
- Question: "Has another bank given you an approval or cash-back offer?"
- WOW: **the approval engine room** — the application moves through real gates (valuation, test-rate serviceability, LVR slot, DTI slot), each opening physically; credit assessor's stamp last.
- Facts: record 6,054 Banking Ombudsman cases 2023-24 (annual report); RBNZ DTI restrictions from 1 Jul 2024 (rbnz.govt.nz); Confirmation of Payee rollout Nov 2024→Easter 2025 (interest.co.nz).
