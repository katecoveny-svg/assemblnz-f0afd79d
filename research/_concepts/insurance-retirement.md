# Sector concepts — Southern Cross · AIG · Retirement living (researched 27 Jul 2026)

## Southern Cross (health insurance)
- Wait: prior approval for elective surgery — request ≥5 working days pre-procedure; ordinary claims ~7 working days. The "is my operation covered?" silence.
- Earn: cover credits per transparent waiting minute, auto-applied to everyday-care claims (GP/dentist/physio).
- Question: "Match you with an Affiliated Provider for this procedure?" (their biggest cost+speed lever).
- WOW: **the approval assembles like a surgical checklist** — quote read line-by-line, each line tested against the plan, lights green onto a building cost breakdown → stamped approval certificate.
- Facts: 5-day prior-approval rule (southerncross.co.nz help); FY25 record 3.8m claims, 94c/premium dollar returned (news/2025); FY25 $56.9m deficit on $1.966b premium, membership 951,808 (chatswood.co.nz).

## AIG NZ (general/travel insurance)
- Wait: travel-claim decision — "10 working days from full documentation" but the real wait is documentation ping-pong; delay = top IFSO complaint theme.
- Earn: excess-back — watched minutes shave the excess (or credit next policy via bank partners).
- Question: "Do you need an emergency payment today?" → interim-payment triage.
- WOW: **the claim rebuilds the trip** — world-map itinerary replay; flights, hospital, receipts pinned to the timeline, pins turn green as evidence verifies. White-label ready (AIG underwrites Westpac Travel Activate).
- Facts: 10-working-day payment commitment (travelactivate.co.nz/claims); IFSO FY25 record 4,293 complaints, disputes +25%, travel ~18% (ifso.nz); AIG NZ since 1970, FMA-licensed (aig.co.nz / fma.govt.nz).

## Retirement living (sector; Ryman/Summerset context)
- Wait: **the exit repayment** — family waits for relicensing before capital returns; average ~4 months, documented 10+ months, fees still accruing, mid-grief.
- Earn: weekly-fee credits — every transparent day past a promised milestone returns that week's fee to the estate.
- Question: "Does your family need part of this money sooner — e.g. care costs?" → early partial release.
- WOW: **"your mum's villa, live"** — the actual unit's relicensing pipeline: refurb, photography, listing, viewings, offer, settlement, each stage stamped, countdown to repayment day.
- Facts: Dec 2025 reform — mandatory repayment ≤12 months, interest after 6; Bill expected Jul 2026 (hud.govt.nz, 1news); Consumer NZ: resident paying fees 10 months after leaving; 83% want ≤3 months (consumer.org.nz); 491 villages, 43,598 units, ~68,000 residents by 2028 (retirement.govt.nz).
