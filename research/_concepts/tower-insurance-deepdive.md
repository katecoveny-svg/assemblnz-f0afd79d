# TOWER INSURANCE — deep dive for the concept demo (researched 27 Jul 2026)

## Snapshot
NZ-owned, NZX/ASX TWR, ~150 yrs, HQ Auckland + Suva hub; NZ+Pacific. FY25: GWP $600m, underlying profit $107.2m record, NPAT $83.7m, 318,000 customers (RNZ 27 Nov 2025). HY26: NPAT $22.9m after $18.5m large-event costs; +15,000 customers YoY, house policies +9% (RNZ 21 May 2026). CEO Paul Johnston credits digital transformation; My Tower self-service, online claims, address-level risk ratings, Ushur straight-through mobile claims. Rotorua office closed Feb 2026 → Auckland+Suva.

## Claims journey + pain (public record)
My Tower lodgement "saves an average of five minutes"; photos validate "twice as fast"; named claims manager; NO published response-time promise. Natural-hazard claims: Tower assesses/settles on behalf of NHC Toka Tū Ake (single point of contact since 30 Jun 2021). 2023 floods+Gabrielle: 8,900 claims = "five years' worth of large loss claims in ~2 weeks" (chief claims officer, Insurance Business 24 Aug 2023); IFSO enquiries record highs, 35% about delays vs 9% norm; Consumer NZ: long silences, assessor churn, 10+-month waits.

## The demo spine
- **Wait that matters**: weather-event HOUSE claim, lodgement → assessment → decision. Tower's own average: **92 days house / 61 contents** (Consumer NZ, Sept 2022–Aug 2023). The void = waiting for assessor + decision with zero visible progress.
- **Earn**: **excess credit** per verified wait milestone, overflow → renewal premium credit. (Tower ran Airpoints-on-premiums 2017–Mar 2024, now discontinued — earning-on-insurance is proven Tower behaviour with a vacant slot.)
- **One question**: **"Is your home safe and liveable tonight?"** → make-safe, temp accommodation, vulnerable-customer, assessor priority.
- **WOW**: **their own risk ratings working mid-claim** — Tower publicly rates every NZ address for flood (RMS on NIWA/LINZ/council data, in the quote flow, twice Canstar-recognised; homepage CSS ships `.range-level-color-1..5`, level 5 #C5093B). The customer's address card lights its real ramp as agents triage.
