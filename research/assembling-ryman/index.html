    assembling · the concept

    The adult child, at 10pm, deciding whether to start the conversation. Public information only. Draft-only. Nothing written to any care system, ever.

    The boundary: no production access is requested by this concept. No resident record, no care plan, no clinical data is involved or asked for. Scope, privacy, security and commercial terms would need joint approval before any pilot began.

    The pilot ask: one village’s guide enquiries, four weeks, read-only public content plus what the family types on the form. Draft-write only to the guide bundle. Scorecard: guide-to-visit rate up, time-to-first-useful-answer down, zero unapproved sends. Fail any line and we change the design or stop.

    Trust wait. The anxious moment is never monetised — Ryman's money is only ever explained for clarity. Measured on enquiry-to-visit and time-to-decision, never on pressure.
