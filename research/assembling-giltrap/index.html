08 — THE WAIT
Four months of nothing, currently

The longest wait in retail, and nobody is using it.

Order a car and you wait months of silence — and silence is where cancellations live. It is also the best window you will ever get to learn what this customer actually needs.

What the customer gets

A credit, not a countdown.

Every stage the car passes through releases a credit toward their ownership — first service, ceramic coating, a towbar, a home charger assessment. Chosen by them, redeemable at the site that sold them the car. It costs less than the discount it replaces, and unlike a discount it brings them back through the door.

What the group gets

A car that arrives already right.

Each stage asks one optional question. By delivery the site knows whether they tow, who else drives it, and whether they can charge at home. Accessories are pre-fitted, the handover is built around them, and the first service is already booked.

✓
One question at a time, all of them optional
No finance data is ever requested. Nothing is passed to a third party. A person confirms every fitting and every booking before it happens.
09 — HOW IT GOES IN
The part most vendors skip

No migration. No new system for anybody to learn.

It sits over the DMS and the systems around it — read-only, beside them, never instead of them. Nothing migrates, IT keeps every key, and it can be switched off again without leaving a hole.

01

A scheduled export

Whatever DMS you run already produces stock, sales and service reports. Point one of them at a folder on a schedule. That is the integration.

No API project · no vendor sign-off
02

A folder of brand assets

Group guidelines and each distributor's CI pack, as they already exist. The system reads them as rules and versions them, so the current pack is always the one in use.

Read-only · nothing is edited
03

Two read-only logins

Meta and GA4, read-only. It watches and recommends; it never spends. Media stays exactly where it is today, with the people who run it today.

No spend authority · ever

Everything else — the creative queue, the meeting notes, the events calendar, the campaign ideas — needs nothing from IT at all. If it is not earning its place in six weeks, the export stops and nothing else changes.

11 — WHAT WE ARE ACTUALLY ASKING
The boundary

What this will never do

It does not send. It drafts, and a named person sends. It does not publish to any channel, write back to your DMS, submit to a distributor portal, book or buy media, commit co-op funds, move money, or price a vehicle. It never touches customer finance data. It records a meeting only with everyone's consent, and any participant can stop it. It does not pretend to be a person — every draft says it was prepared by a machine and approved by a human.

No production access is requested by this concept.
