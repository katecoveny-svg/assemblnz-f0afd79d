01 · the scope
One billing cycle · 500–1,000 opted-in Good Nights customers in Auckland · a 4-week sandbox.
02 · the access
Read-only meter, weather and tariff data. Draft-write only for the bill-explanation card. No writes to the billing ledger in phase 1 — staged credit for phase-2 review.
03 · the scorecard
≥15pt uplift in bill understanding · ≥40% call deflection for "why is my bill this?" · ≥30% opt-in rate · 0 unapproved actions.

fail any line of the scorecard and we change the design or stop.

the boundary

No production access is requested by this concept. No live customer, price, reward or purchase is involved. Scope, privacy, security and commercial terms require joint approval before any pilot begins.
