the pilot ask

the smallest credible next step.

01 · the scope
One disruption class (weather delay >4h at ZQN or WLG) · 500–1,000 opted-in Airpoints Silver+ members based in Auckland · a 4-week window.
02 · the access
Read-only schedule and passenger context. Draft-write only for rebook proposals. No writes to reservations in phase 1 — staged rebook for phase-2 review.
03 · the scorecard
≥50% call deflection · ≥60% three-option review rate · ≥40% confirmed in-app rebook · 0 unapproved actions.

fail any line of the scorecard and we change the design or stop.

the boundary

No production access is requested by this concept. No live customer, price, reward or purchase is involved. Scope, privacy, security and commercial terms require joint approval before any pilot begins.
