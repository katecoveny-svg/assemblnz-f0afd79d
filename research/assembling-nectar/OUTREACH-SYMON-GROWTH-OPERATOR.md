# Nectar × Assembl — Symon outreach

## Core proposition

Nectar is already hiring for a Performance Marketing Specialist with ownership across paid acquisition, SEO/GEO, CRO, audience strategy, attribution, CAC, lead quality, approvals and funded outcomes.

Assembl should not pitch this as “AI marketing support”. The pilot proposition is stronger:

> **Before Nectar commits to the permanent hire, give Assembl the workload for 30 days.**
>
> Assembl runs the repetitive performance layer, joins acquisition to downstream lending outcomes, and operates a live applicant agent across the individual customer journey. Nectar keeps strategy, regulated judgement and lending decisions human-owned.

The goal is not to claim every part of a senior marketing role can be removed. The goal is to prove how much of the advertised operational workload can be covered by an agentic operating layer before Nectar fixes the permanent operating model.

## Demo

**Personalised URL:**
https://assembling-nectar.pages.dev/journey-v3.html?for=symon

The experience has two modes:

1. **customer journey** — live applicant agent from application to funded outcome.
2. **growth operator** — live performance agent from paid media to funded outcome.

The two are deliberately connected. The customer agent sees friction in individual journeys. The growth operator sees aggregate patterns and can prepare or execute bounded changes to media, audiences, landing pages, suppression, CRO and reporting.

## 30-day pilot ask

### Scope
- Google Ads, Meta and Microsoft Ads read access first.
- Analytics / landing-page conversion events.
- Campaign / source ID carried into the application journey.
- Aggregate states for application, assessable, approved and funded.
- Current landing pages, creative library and brand/compliance rules.
- Optional bounded write access after recommendations have been observed and approved.

### What Assembl runs
- daily account monitoring and anomaly detection;
- spend and CAC pacing;
- search-term / keyword waste and opportunity analysis;
- audience quality analysis;
- retargeting and suppression recommendations;
- landing-page / funnel diagnostics;
- CRO experiment briefs and variants;
- SEO / GEO opportunity monitoring and briefs;
- attribution from spend to funded outcome;
- lead-quality analysis;
- commercial recommendations;
- bounded campaign / budget actions after authority is agreed;
- live applicant-agent journey for one defined application wait state.

### Human-owned
- lending decisions;
- responsible-lending / compliance accountability;
- major budget and positioning decisions;
- final brand judgement;
- any action outside the agreed authority envelope.

### Scorecard
- manual analyst / reporting hours removed;
- percentage of repetitive role tasks covered;
- recommendations accepted vs rejected;
- time from performance issue to prepared action;
- visibility from channel to assessable / approved / funded outcome;
- landing / funnel experiments launched;
- reduction in irrelevant retargeting;
- applicant waits resolved by the customer agent;
- funded-outcome economics, with no weakening of compliance or customer-quality controls.

## Positioning discipline

Do **not** say “Assembl replaces a marketer”.

Say:

> **Nectar can test whether it needs this permanent operating model before it hires it.**

That is more credible, more commercially provocative, and easier for Symon to say yes to.
