the boundary panel · every rule real

What this journey refuses to do.

Ported unchanged from the concept’s rulebook — the refusals are the product, and the strongest output is the one that declines to produce a message.

×No rate predicted, ever

It explains what builds a personalised rate using published factors, and never estimates the number. That is the engine’s output and only its output.

CCCFA 2003
×No credit decision, anywhere

No output says approved, declined, pre-approved, eligible or likely. Every one ends with a person, not an outcome.

CCCFA · Responsible Lending Code
×Hardship stops everything

Language that reads as hardship means nothing is drafted at all. Routed to a named person with the triggering words attached. One right answer is no loan.

CCCFA · lender responsibilities
×The fee credit is pricing, not bait

A complete file costs less to process, so it can cost less — capped well below anything that could move an affordability decision.

CCCFA s41 · FMA fee guidance
×No third party sees anything

Nothing is passed to anyone else. Separate businesses need separate consent, and this concept does not bridge them.
