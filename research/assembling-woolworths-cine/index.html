the pilot ask

the smallest credible next step.

01 · the scope
One weekly-online-shop journey · 500–1,000 opted-in Everyday Rewards members · a 4-week sandbox.
02 · the access
Read-only catalogue + household context. Draft-write only for the basket. No writes to the rewards ledger in phase 1 — staged credit for phase-2 review.
03 · the scorecard
≥30 min returned per member per week · ≥60% completion of opted-in wait moments · ≥30% basket-review rate · 0 unapproved actions.

fail any line of the scorecard and we change the design or stop.

the boundary

No production access is requested by this concept. No live customer, price, reward or purchase is involved. Scope, privacy, security and commercial terms require joint approval before any pilot begins.
