# Outreach — the demo fleet, 27 July 2026

Every approach is a **signed link to a working page**, sent to the person who owns
the patch. No deck, no "book a demo". Each page ends the same way: the boundary,
the pilot ask (scope · access · scorecard), and three reply-verbs — correct us,
narrow it, decline it.

**`?for=` signs the page.** The ribbon at the top changes to the recipient's
title, so the page reads as prepared for them. Keys are per demo, listed below.

**Before you send any of these:** the pages are labelled independent concepts and
carry no client data. But six of them still have **no verified buyer** — a name
and exact title, checked this week. Sending to a guessed name is worse than not
sending. Those are flagged 🔎.

**Two palettes are unverified** and should not go out until you've eyeballed them:
Electric Kiwi and Trade Me. Both flagged ⚠️.

**Use them for:** the "show me what this looks like in my industry" reply, before
you've built anything named. Send the category, not a pitch:

> You asked what this looks like in practice. Here's the shape in your category —

> [link]

They're also the safe answer to "do you have anything I can share internally?"
Nothing on them can be mistaken for a competitor's confidential work.
