# Assembl LinkedIn Launch Campaign

## Week 1: The thesis
1. Proof, not just automation. AI can draft fast. Serious work needs context, review, and a trail.
2. Agents do the mahi. People own the outcome. That is Assembl's operating principle.
3. The next AI advantage in New Zealand will be workflow trust, not more chatbots.
4. What is an evidence pack? Sources, assumptions, checks, review notes, timestamps, and decision path.
5. Built in Aotearoa because context matters.

## Week 2: The workflows
6. Construction: RFIs, QA checks, programme risks, site observations.
7. Hospitality: food safety, incidents, onboarding, event readiness.
8. Logistics: freight exceptions, customs checks, customer updates.
9. Education: newsletters, dates, gear lists, parent comms.
10. Public sector: transparency by design, not as an afterthought.

## Week 3: The proof layer
11. Compliance is not the product. Trust is.
12. Human review is not friction. It is how agentic systems become usable.
13. A good agent workflow should be able to show its working.
14. Start with one process. Prove the value. Then scale.
15. The evidence pack is the handover between automation and accountability.

## Week 4: The offer
16. Give us one workflow. We will turn it into proof with a focused pilot sprint.
17. What a Pilot Sprint includes: mapping, agent workflow, review points, evidence pack, handover.
18. Why vessels? They hold context, work, review, and evidence together.
19. NZ businesses do not need AI theatre. They need operational proof.
20. Mahi that earns its proof. Built in Aotearoa.
