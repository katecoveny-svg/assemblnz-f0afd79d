## Purpose
Build the production-ready Assembl website and launch asset system for assembl.co.nz.

## Core message
Mahi that earns its proof.

## Positioning
Assembl is a New Zealand platform for specialist agents that run operational mahi with human review and evidence packs.

Use "agent-assisted" not "AI-assisted".

## Pricing canon
- Pilot Sprint: from $5,000 + GST
- Industry Pack: $5,000/month
- Outcome pricing: from $5,000+ for custom multi-kete work

Do not lock the public Pilot Sprint offer to a fixed 10-working-day delivery promise unless the founder explicitly re-approves it.

## Contact email

Avoid public status labels such as Live / Pilot / Greenfield on marketing cards unless explicitly requested. The public site should feel premium and confident, not like an internal roadmap.

## Visual direction
Warm paper background (#FAF7F2), pounamu green (#176B4D), soft brass (#D4A853), dark warm taupe (#23211F), soft taupe (#6F6158). Use Cormorant Garamond or EB Garamond for headlines, Inter for UI, IBM Plex Mono for metadata.

## Asset language
Use delicate evidence vessels: translucent glass, ceramic, pounamu, amber, violet, teal, smoky glass, thin brass frames, soft natural light, museum-quiet restraint.

Avoid robots, neon AI visuals, fake dashboard clutter, generic SaaS gradients, stock-photo energy, and baked-in nav bars on OG cards.
