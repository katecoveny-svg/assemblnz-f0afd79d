work companion.

Place DO on the page you are working in. Move it, point to an area, then review the context before asking it to work.

Nothing is captured or recorded until you choose it. Provider use needs separate consent in DO.

What travels with me?

The floating workspace can read an area only after you choose Point at an area and click. Hover previews stay on the page. Forms and marked private areas are excluded. Moving DO never starts capture. The separate browser window uses your normal Assembl session. An explicitly selected text excerpt can be placed in the draft editor for review. This is not a background agent, cross-device job sync or permission to act.

Sites that block embedded workspaces can use Open DO workspace and copy/paste instead. Share a screen takes one snapshot you can review. Drag the browser window by its title bar. It is not an always-on-top native overlay. Voice and meetings ask for microphone access on the first-party page.
