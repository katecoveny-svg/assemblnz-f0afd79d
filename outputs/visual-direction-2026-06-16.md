# Visual direction — 2026-06-16 (Kate)

North-star visual language for the assembl marketing site. Source: Kate's one-image
direction (`outputs/visual-direction-2026-06-16.png`). This doc is the brief that the
imagery-placement survey (`outputs/visual-interest-survey-2026-06-16.md`) lands into.
**Survey + direction only — no imagery generated yet.**

The reference mockup reads, top to bottom: dark pounamu hero (vessel + "Mahi that earns
its proof.") → cream "less admin, more mahi" with a big stat row → dark "From work to
proof" with vessel → cream kete-pack card grid → NZ coast photo + founder-at-desk photo
side by side → dark footer with a large `assembl` wordmark.

---

## The eight design moves

1. **Alternating dark/cream section rhythm.** Dark pounamu (`#0E5546` / `#2B6B57`) and
   cream (`#FAF7F2` / `#F7F3EE`) backgrounds alternate down the page to break wordiness
   into measured movements. **See refinement 1 — cream is the base, dark is the accent.**
2. **Vessel renders as hero accents.** Translucent green spiral forms with a gold
   ember/glow at the base — feather-like and luminous, *not* the old wireframe stack.
   **See refinement 2 — the glints are the metaphor.**
3. **Bold cream Cormorant serif headlines on dark green.** Reserved for the
   high-contrast emphasis moments (hero, the close). Big, light-weight, generous.
4. **Real NZ landscape photography at section breaks** — coast, mountains, native bush.
   Grounds the work in Aotearoa; used as full-bleed or half-bleed breaks, not decoration.
5. **Real human photography in working context** — founder/team at the desk, on site —
   for the "Let's build what's next" close and the About moment. Warmth and credibility.
6. **Big statistics displayed prominently** — `35K` / `$8M` / `98%` / `100%`-style number
   callouts, large numeral over a short label. A fast, scannable proof rhythm.
7. **Clean kete-pack card grids in cream sections** — light, orderly, generous spacing;
   the calm counterweight to the dark emphasis bands.
8. **Dark footer with a prominent `assembl` wordmark** — lowercase, large, the brand
   signature closing every page.

### Palette tokens
- Dark pounamu: `#0E5546` (deep) · `#2B6B57` (standard) — emphasis backgrounds
- Cream: `#FAF7F2` (paper) · `#F7F3EE` (warm) — base backgrounds
- Gold ember: vessel-base glow / data-node glints (use existing Arataki amber `#D9A85A` / brass `#B8964F` family)
- Cream Cormorant headlines on dark; ink (`#3D4250`) body on cream

---

## Refinement 1 — dark in measured doses, not dominant

"Bits of dark." **Cream is the base; dark pounamu is for moments of emphasis** — the hero,
key stat callouts, and the "Let's build what's next" close — not the default. The page
should feel warm and light overall, with the dark bands as punctuation. A good rule of
thumb: **roughly one dark band to every two cream sections**, and never two dark sections
back to back unless one is the hero and the other is the footer.

## Refinement 2 — the vessel sparkles ARE the metaphor

The translucent glints on the vessel are **data nodes** — each glint is a piece of
evidence captured and held in the vessel. **Vessel = the evidence pack; sparkles = the
data being held.** Anywhere this survey or a future prompt calls for a vessel render,
describe the sparkles intentionally as **"gold data-node glints"** (evidence captured in
the vessel) so the generation carries the concept, not just the look. The vessel isn't
decoration — it's the product promise rendered.
